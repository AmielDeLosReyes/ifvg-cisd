[b]SMT Creative Trader[/b]

[b]What It Is[/b]

Most of the time, correlated instruments like NQ and ES move together. When one makes a new high, the other usually does too. But sometimes they disagree. One sweeps a level and the other doesn't follow. That disagreement is called SMT divergence, and it's one of the most reliable signals that a move is weak and a reversal is likely.

[b]How It Works[/b]

The indicator watches both instruments and compares their swing highs and swing lows. When one breaks a level and the other doesn't confirm, it draws a line connecting the divergence so you can see it instantly.

[i]Bullish divergence[/i]: one instrument makes a lower low while the other holds a higher low. Selling pressure is weakening.

[i]Bearish divergence:[/i] one instrument makes a higher high while the other makes a lower high. Buying pressure is weakening.

[b]What Makes This Different[/b]

Most SMT indicators update after the candle closes. By then, the move already happened. This one updates in real time as price moves. You see the divergence forming while the candle is still printing.

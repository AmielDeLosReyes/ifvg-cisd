=== [2022 Ep3 — 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 3 (ID nQfHZ2DEJ8c)] — Premium / discount; targets & partials (low-hanging fruit)
- **Kind:** rule (applied)
- **What ICT says:** Bearish leg: price goes from premium to below the 50% (equilibrium) of the range into discount, into the gap there. Partials can be taken below each short-term low (sell stops), but don't take them where there's too little movement; reach for the next one. First objective = the high end of the target FVG (the closest candle's low/high), the easiest target; over time grow into holding to see it fill the whole gap.
- **How to identify:** 50% of the leg's high and low; FVG/imbalance below.
- **How to use:** Multiple contracts: scale out at successive sell-stop pools and the FVG in discount. Live trade: exit at the low end of the target rectangle; the ideal is the top, but while learning take the low end.
- **Example in this video:** NQ 2m bearish leg to FVG below 50%; live long target = bottom of pink imbalance in premium.
- **Timestamps:** [0:17:43](https://youtu.be/nQfHZ2DEJ8c?t=1063) — targets below; partials; [0:19:20](https://youtu.be/nQfHZ2DEJ8c?t=1160) — 50% level, first objective in gap, low-hanging fruit; [0:27:16](https://youtu.be/nQfHZ2DEJ8c?t=1636) — premium to equilibrium to discount; [0:49:07](https://youtu.be/nQfHZ2DEJ8c?t=2947) — target low end of rectangle.

=== [2022 Ep4 — 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 4 (ID L-ReMHiavPM)] — Range, Fibonacci equilibrium and target selection  (aliases ICT uses: "equilibrium", "50 level", "discount")
- **Kind:** rule (applied)
- **What ICT says:** Define the range from a chosen low to the high of the run; drop a Fib on it; the 50% is equilibrium. When the run pushes price into premium (expensive), wait for the bearish shift; target a low or an imbalance below equilibrium.
- **How to identify:** Low of the range to high of the range; 50% level.
- **How to use:** Short targets: swing low or imbalance below the 50%; multiple targets can be taken in sequence.
- **Example in this video:** ES March 2022, Wed Jan 26 2022, 2m: range from a marked low to the high; after the run up into premium, a swing low and an imbalance below the 50% serve as targets; price hits the imbalance, bounces, then draws down into the more pronounced low.

=== [2022 Ep7 — 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 7 (ID G8-z91acgG4)] — Dealing range, premium/discount and consolidation at equilibrium ("consolidation hurdles")
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:**
  - Once sell-side liquidity below the range low has been taken, price is likely to retrace back inside the range (the swing high to the swing low).
  - In the example price went back up into premium (above equilibrium), then down into discount (bounced off the old low), then consolidated around equilibrium.
  - When price hangs around equilibrium of the daily range, bias is very hard to read, even for him. That doesn't mean no trading. It means:
    - relying on intraday charts,
    - looking for liquidity pools,
    - trading intraday volatility (runs on old highs and old lows),
    - being nimble, a "surgical strike": take your handles and get out, don't overstay.
- **How to identify:** On the daily chart, mark the high to the low of the most recent swing after the liquidity raid and use a Fib only for the midpoint (equilibrium). If price is oscillating around 50%, you're in a consolidation hurdle.
- **How to use:** Lower your expectations, take intraday liquidity targets only, and use smaller objectives.
- **Example in this video:** NQ daily after the sell-side raid. Price retraced into premium, then discount, then chopped around equilibrium. ICT preferred a move lower for Tuesday 8 Feb 2022 (a sweep of the lows) but it didn't deliver.
- **Timestamps:** [0:01:50](https://youtu.be/G8-z91acgG4?t=110) — premium, discount, then consolidation around equilibrium · [0:10:37](https://youtu.be/G8-z91acgG4?t=637) — what to do when bias is unclear
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** He uses the Fib only to find the midpoint, saying "no OTE, no indicators" in this mentorship.

=== [2022 Ep7 — 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 7 (ID G8-z91acgG4)] — Leader orders / test contracts at the open  (aliases ICT uses: "leaders", "tossing a contract in", "premium for Intel")
- **Kind:** refinement (execution technique)
- **What ICT says:**
  - When he has no read (e.g. a sloppy 9:30 open, price in mid-range on the daily), he puts in a single contract, long or short, just to feel how price delivers around it.
  - Watch whether price moves easily away from the order, moves lethargically, or runs straight against it. If the test order is "ran over", your bias probably isn't aligned.
  - Small losses on these are "premium payments" for information. They aren't skill errors.
  - He learned this from a floor trader and George Angell (S&P floor traders). He also does it in Forex.
  - A demo account can't give this feedback loop because nothing is at risk.
  - He's not trying to pick a spot when he tosses these in, and he doesn't do it every day.
  - The 9:30 open is characteristically sloppy. Many people say don't trade it; he doesn't mind it.
- **Example in this video:** 8 Feb 2022. Two leader orders plus a short around the open lost money (one cost about $750). The 10:36 long, 14,505.5 to 14,622.75, made $2,345 on 1 NQ mini, which covered the leaders and netted about $1,100 for the day.

=== [2022 Ep8 — 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 8 (ID 7rbV8aWkcqY)] — Fib standard deviation (−1) projection for targets in expansion  (aliases ICT uses: standard deviation negative one)
- **Kind:** rule / refinement
- **What ICT says:**
  - When the setup forms in the middle of a range, with no nearby old high or low to target ("if we don't have a range to trade inside of"), project how far an expansion can go.
  - Anchor a Fibonacci on the previous swing: the low to the high of the price leg right after price leaves the consolidation (before the discount retracement). Then use the −1 standard deviation as the target.
  - His Fib settings are in the "OTE primer" video on the channel.
- **How to identify:** The swing that broke structure before the consolidation and pullback. The pullback creates the discount, and then the retracement rally.
- **How to use:** The −1 extension of that swing is the expansion target. Here it was hit "right to the point".
- **Example in this video:** EURJPY. The −1 SD projection landed at about 133.15/133.153, which matched the high of the move (133.153), and price reacted from there.
- **Timestamps:** [0:08:00](https://youtu.be/7rbV8aWkcqY?t=480) — no range to target, use the swing leg · [0:08:46](https://youtu.be/7rbV8aWkcqY?t=526) — anchor Fib, −1 SD, 133.153
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** A harder-timeframe old high exists further left, but the SD projection is the "real easy way" when nothing is close.

=== [2022 Ep9 — 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 9 (ID iZLXnNiZm_s)] — Daily dealing range: discount plus an indecisive daily candle means expect a retracement
- **Kind:** rule / example
- **What ICT says:**
  - Choose the daily range: the most recent swing low (which took out a short-term low, digging into SSL) and the swing high. Fib it only for the 50% (equilibrium). Below 50% is discount.
  - On Monday 14 Feb 2022 NQ traded into a deep discount but didn't take the lows further left. Monday closed with an indecisive candle, the body "basically absent" (open ≈ close).
  - In discount with an indecisive close, a retracement may be needed first, even if a lower low is the eventual target.
  - Monday's close left a daily imbalance (FVG). Overnight into Tuesday, price ran up into it. The FVG's lower and upper edges became reference levels on the lower timeframes.
- **Example in this video:** NQ continuous (TradingView). Mon 14 Feb: deep discount, doji-like close, daily FVG. Tue 15 Feb: overnight run into the FVG; the upper end of the FVG was the draw.

=== [2022 Ep19 — 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 19 (ID IEa1N0rTtbc)] — Daily bias from equilibrium of the dealing range  (aliases: daily bias, short-term discount)
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** If the daily chart retraces to ~50% (equilibrium / short-term discount) of its dealing range, there's a likelihood the next day (or the day after) will be bullish. You go into the next day expecting higher prices; if the next day is down or flat you still expect higher. Then identify the premium array the market will draw to (e.g., a daily bearish order block above) and pick the easiest target.
- **How to identify:** Daily dealing range → 50% hit → look above for the premium PD array (OB low / open / mean threshold / high) and for old highs / relative equal highs.
- **How to use:** You're not predicting the daily close; you want to participate in the *morning-session* move in the bias direction into the target, not the whole leg.
- **Example in this video:** ES June 2022 daily: market traded into a deep discount, retraced to ~50% of the dealing range from the most recent swing low; next-day bias bullish; premium array = last up-close daily candle before the down-displacement (bearish OB), targets its low, body, mean threshold, high. Buying below the opening price on the bullish day could reach the candle's low (easiest), body, and the mean threshold.
- **Timestamps:** [1:18:37](https://youtu.be/IEa1N0rTtbc?t=4717) — equilibrium → bullish bias; [1:19:24](https://youtu.be/IEa1N0rTtbc?t=4764) — "you want to be taught about bias, I'm teaching it"; [1:20:10](https://youtu.be/IEa1N0rTtbc?t=4810) — premium array; [1:21:47](https://youtu.be/IEa1N0rTtbc?t=4907) — pick the easiest target.
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** Even if the market eventually goes lower, the intraday "surgical strike" up into liquidity still works; he doesn't care about the longer-term direction for the intraday trade.

=== [2022 Ep25 — 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 25 (ID CnTXwAuDi9Y)] — Rebalancing a large daily range (previous-day low as retracement target)
- **Kind:** rule / new framing
- **What ICT says:** After a large range down day (Monday, closed near its low), go back to the *previous day's* low (Friday's low). The next day's retracement up to that level "rebalances" Monday's entire range/sell-off. It tricks people into thinking the low is in, when price has only gone up to a logical level on the daily that rebalances the drop. On the 15m the move doesn't look like an imbalance, but on the daily it's a large distribution candle.
- **How to identify:** Large down day closing near low → mark prior day's low → expect the next session's Judas rally to reach it (when bias is bearish).
- **How to use:** Use the prior day's low as the "how far can it go" upper limit for the Judas swing; look for the short setup there.
- **Example in this video:** ES, Tue May 10 2022: price ran up from 8:30 and hit Friday's (May 6) low right at the 9:30 candle, rebalancing Monday (May 9)'s drop, then sold off.
- **Timestamps:** [0:21:24](https://youtu.be/CnTXwAuDi9Y?t=1284) — go back to previous day's low; [0:31:48](https://youtu.be/CnTXwAuDi9Y?t=1908) — rebalances Monday's range; [0:32:36](https://youtu.be/CnTXwAuDi9Y?t=1956) — "how far can it go — that's the Friday low."

=== [2026 #125 — ICT 2026 Market Commentary \ March 08, 2026 (ID JIs96_2rfkE)] — Discount Sensitivity vs Premium Sensitivity (Price Action Behavior)
- **Kind:** definition / rule
- **What ICT says:** Discount array = inefficiency below current price (from down-close candle). When price retraces into discount array, "discount sensitivity" means bodies should stay in UPPER half of the inefficiency. If bodies breach lower half, discount is lost. Premium array = inefficiency above price. Premium sensitivity = bodies must stay in LOWER half. Sensitivity = the boundary that bodies must respect to maintain bullish/bearish control.
- **How to identify:** Mark FVG. Identify if it's above (premium) or below (discount) current price. When price retraces, watch if body respects the boundary. Upper half vs lower half = midpoint (CE).
- **How to use:** In discount array, if retracement bodies stay above CE, it's bullish. If they breach below CE on close, discount is lost and bear control is in question. Same logic reversed for premium. Use this to confirm or invalidate your bias.
- **Example in this video:** ES March 09: FVG formed (premium = above price). When price retraced, bodies came DOWN into it but didn't close below CE = bullish, discount sensitivity held. Price then ripped higher.
- **Timestamps:** [0:26:09](https://youtu.be/JIs96_2rfkE?t=1569) — explaining discount sensitivity on S&P; [0:27:47](https://youtu.be/JIs96_2rfkE?t=1667) — "Bullish order flow is going to respect the upper boundaries of a discount array"
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** This is the CORE of reading order flow without looking "inside the candle" at tick data or volume profiles. Bodies + levels = pure order flow.

=== [2024 #2 — ICT 2024 Mentorship \ Lecture #2   August 6, 2024 (ID pkI4AgC4OPI)] — FVG classification & annotation (SIBI / BISI)  (aliases: inefficiencies, imbalances)
- **Kind:** definition / rule
- **What ICT says:**
  - The middle candle's close names the gap. A **down-close** middle candle = **SIBI** (sell-side imbalance, buy-side inefficiency): it offered delivery only on the downside and is inefficient for any move back up. An **up-close** middle candle = **BISI** (buy-side imbalance, sell-side inefficiency).
  - SIBI definition: a down-close candle where the previous candle's low and the next candle's high don't connect. The only bridge between them is the down-close candle.
  - Everything from the first candle's low to the third candle's high is inefficient and "invites" price to trade back into it. That doesn't mean now, or even this session.
  - **Keep the original FVG levels** (low, consequent encroachment/midpoint, high) even after partial fills, because they're used again. Mentally note the **remaining unfilled portion**. Tiny remaining slivers matter: ICT has placed limit orders just beyond such slivers and had them run with no significant drawdown.
  - If price later revisits, it may just tap the high of the gap. If it goes above and comes back down, he uses the candle's wick and its halfway point as the retracement limit, and the gap becomes an inversion FVG (caption somewhat unclear here).
  - "These inefficiencies are the real support and resistance": price kisses them, turns on a dime, and bodies stop at them.
  - Color scheme is up to you. He won't share his exact colors, so copycats can't pass his charts off as theirs.
- **How to identify:** After checking for relative equal highs/lows on 15m, 5m and 1m (or finding none), annotate all inefficiencies, starting on the 1m and working back up.
- **Timestamps:** [1:03:11](https://youtu.be/pkI4AgC4OPI?t=3791) annotate inefficiencies once the relative equal highs/lows check is done; down-close = SIBI. [1:04:49](https://youtu.be/pkI4AgC4OPI?t=3889) small remaining gaps used for limit orders. [1:05:39](https://youtu.be/pkI4AgC4OPI?t=3939) keep original levels, note the remaining portion. === [2024 #3 — ICT 2024 Mentorship \ Lecture #3   August 7, 2024 (ID ZkLc4-adodg)] — Consequent Encroachment (CE) vs. Equilibrium; gap quadrants  (aliases: midpoint, "consequent encroachment")
- **Kind:** definition + refinement
- **What ICT says:**
  - **CE is the midpoint of any gap or inefficiency.** It is not called equilibrium. **Equilibrium** belongs to a *range* that has been delivered up and down (a trading range). A gap is not a range: no trades printed between the 5 PM stop and the 6 PM restart.
  - Grade the gap with 0.25 / 0.5 / 0.75 to get equal quarters. Price respects the upper and lower quadrants to the tick.
  - **SIBI (down-close FVG):** the best shorts form in the **lower half**, and you want the upper half left open. **BISI (up-close FVG):** the best longs fill in the **upper half**, leaving the lower half open. When price can't fill that half and runs, it is a **breakaway gap**. That tells you the algorithm will move quickly, so anticipate large range expansion.
- **How to use:** CE serves as an entry level and as a **strength/weakness gauge** (see the order block concept below).
- **Timestamps:** [0:31:04](https://youtu.be/ZkLc4-adodg?t=1864) CE vs equilibrium · [0:33:25](https://youtu.be/ZkLc4-adodg?t=2005) CE rules for SIBI/BISI, breakaway gap

=== [2024 #6 — ICT 2024 Mentorship \ Lecture #6   August 12, 2024 Begins 9:15am ET (ID woAQ1XJeD7Y)] — FVG measurement with volume imbalance, and "upper half stays open" as the algorithmic signature  (aliases: respecting half of a gap, SIBI/BISI, "bid/sell efficiency")

=== [2026 #136 — ICT 2026 Smart Money Concepts Lecture \ January 29, 2026 (ID 8wHewi6N6dg)] — Fair Value Gap as a "two SIBIs plus a volume imbalance" array; how to draw it and test it  (aliases ICT uses: SIBI, sell-side imbalance buy-side inefficiency, volume imbalance, inefficiency, PD array, "split")

- **What ICT says:**

=== [2022 Ep23 — 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 23 (ID TV9TfwHXuqs)] — FOMC / news-event delivery pattern  (aliases: FOMC, knee-jerk reaction, NFP-style legs, 2:00 / 2:30 FOMC)
- **Kind:** rule / entry model
- **What ICT says:** FOMC behaves like non-farm payroll: a knee-jerk initial leg, then another leg, then a fake-out leg, *then* the real setup comes. He waits a few minutes for the "initial shakeout" before outlining anything. The announcement is at 2:00 p.m.; price drops into the level, and usually by around 2:30 the initial leg that "prices in" the event (high or low) is finished and the real move starts. A trade taken ahead of the FOMC announcement is a "gambler's setup" and he doesn't count it.
- **How to use:** Mark where the initial leg should terminate (a discount FVG below 50% of the range, near sell-side liquidity), wait for price to deliver into it after 2:00, then look for the reversal and the run on the opposite liquidity (here Tuesday's high).
- **Example in this video:** MNQ: after 2:00 the market drops; ICT marks the blue zone as the end of the bears' race; price drops into it shortly after 2:30, turns, and runs Tuesday's high.
- **Timestamps:** [0:00:06](https://youtu.be/TV9TfwHXuqs?t=6) — waiting for FOMC initial shakeout, "end of the road" zone; [0:01:40](https://youtu.be/TV9TfwHXuqs?t=100) — FOMC like NFP: leg, leg, fake-out leg, then real setup; [0:11:10](https://youtu.be/TV9TfwHXuqs?t=670) — 2:00 drop, initial leg finished ~2:30, then rally; [0:14:23](https://youtu.be/TV9TfwHXuqs?t=863) — pre-FOMC trade = gambler's setup.
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** "This isn't an invitation" to trade FOMC or NFP — extremely risky; great to study in hindsight or watch live as a case study of liquidity runs.

=== [2024 #1 — ICT 2024 Mentorship \ Lecture #1  August 5, 2024 (ID GKeLVR3dPuI)] — Market standard delivery vs. manual intervention
- **Kind:** definition
- **What ICT says:**
  - Normal algorithmic delivery goes up a little and comes back a little.
  - Manual intervention is FOMC, CPI or NFP-type delivery: one direction, no pause. If you're on the wrong side when it hits, "you're dead". It is often untradeable once it starts ("chasing the wind").
  - Manual intervention (and geopolitical shocks) is the one thing that always makes his model wrong, and nobody can predict it. That is the main reason never to over-leverage.
  - Gaps can jump far beyond your stop, which is why brokers make you sign risk disclosures.

=== [2024 #3 — ICT 2024 Mentorship \ Lecture #3   August 7, 2024 (ID ZkLc4-adodg)] — Trading ahead of a news release (pre-1:00 logic)
- **Kind:** rule / example
- **What ICT says:** People ask how he knows direction before news. His answer: if price has already rounded over, left an FVG whose **candle bodies respect it**, and started **displacing lower** before the release, the likelihood is that it will explore below the nearby lows, because that's where liquidity is. Buying and selling after the release does not cause the move. The market is already running before the number comes out.
- **Example in this video:** NQ 1m. At **12:42 ET**, price trades up and rolls over, leaving an FVG ("one of the 81 PD arrays") with bodies respecting it, then displaces lower going into the 1:00 auction. He lists the draws below:
  - (1) a short-term low, the day-session low since 7:00, with minor SSL
  - (2) the London session low, a relative equal low that passes the right-side-higher rule
  - (3) the NDOG further down as the "black hole"
- **Timestamps:** [1:34:20](https://youtu.be/ZkLc4-adodg?t=5660) 12:42 FVG roll-over · [1:36:44](https://youtu.be/ZkLc4-adodg?t=5804) the logic before news · [1:39:12](https://youtu.be/ZkLc4-adodg?t=5952) three draws below

=== [2024 #24 — ICT 2024 Mentorship \ NonFarm Payroll September 06, 2024 (ID MxaJD1WIzos)] — News drivers: NFP / FOMC / CPI / PPI handling  (aliases ICT uses: "manual intervention", high/medium-impact news driver)
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:**
  - NFP is "not algorithmic": like FOMC (and CPI, PPI) it is a manual intervention, where "they literally pick where they want it to go abruptly". The same logic (run to liquidity / inefficiency) still applies, it is just delivered abruptly and farther outside the normal range.
  - Do not try to position ahead of it (he would have terrible stats; he does not know where they will run it first). Do not trade the release itself. Earliest anyone should trade NFP is about 15 minutes after the 8:30 release (8:45 ET); 20-30 minutes after is better. If already in a trade you can leave a small runner.
  - After the initial shock, look at what is "left behind" (smooth edges / inefficiencies / untouched pools); that is what price goes for next.
  - Why you cannot trade the first minutes: slippage (his example: aiming to buy 18,845 and getting filled 18,862 or worse, which blows up your risk), requotes, or no fill at all. Brokers "pull their engagement" because retail over-leverages funded accounts into these events; this is the illusion of "illiquidity" (liquidity exists, brokers withhold execution). A limit at the opening price of the run +1 tick can fill about 20 minutes after, not in the first ~3 minutes.
  - Use NFP as a clue only: framing the 15-min pools beforehand tells you which pool is more likely to be run (see next concept). He is "not always right"; on CPI/PPI he says his anticipated direction of the initial delivery is right only ~2% of the time (98% wrong) (as stated).
  - Medium/high impact only: he uses Econoday (since the 1990s) and Forex Factory; ignores low-impact; cares about US data, Fed-related events (caption unclear) and bond auctions; speeches matter less. Fundamentals/raw numbers (jobs created or lost) are irrelevant ("the chart's going to tell you everything"); the data is "meant to stimulate" investors into risking money.
- **How to use:** Pre-frame buy-side/sell-side on the 15-min before 8:30, note which is favored, do NOT enter, wait for the shock, then re-read.
- **Example in this video:** NQ, Fri Sept 6, 2024. Before 8:30 price had already traded down to the Aug 13 daily FVG low (~18,765, his target; he had covered his short there). NFP then ran up: first to a minor 5-min buy-side level, jammed down taking a short-term low without breaking the main 15-min low, then ripped through the buy side (a single 5m candle; wicking around on the 1m) and into the New Day Opening Gap before 9:30.
- **Timestamps:** [0:08:01](https://youtu.be/MxaJD1WIzos?t=481) NFP not algorithmic, wait 15-30 min; [0:09:38](https://youtu.be/MxaJD1WIzos?t=578) wait for the shock, see what is left behind; [0:13:47](https://youtu.be/MxaJD1WIzos?t=827) brokers pull liquidity / illiquidity illusion; [0:55:22](https://youtu.be/MxaJD1WIzos?t=3322) slippage and requotes
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** Consistent with his earlier "don't trade news" line; the new angle is using NFP as a liquidity-selection clue and the explicit 15-minute earliest-entry rule.

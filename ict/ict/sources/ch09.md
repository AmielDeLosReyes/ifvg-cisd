=== [2022 Ep3 — 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 3 (ID nQfHZ2DEJ8c)] — Order block = change in the state of delivery  (aliases ICT uses: "one continuous order block", "consecutive bearish order block", "change in the state of delivery")
- **Kind:** definition / refinement
- **What ICT says:** He invented the order block (first mentioned on BabyPips in 2010; taught one-on-one since 1996). Not every down-close candle is a bullish OB (or up-close a bearish OB). A series of consecutive down-close candles is "all one continuous order block"; the reference is the opening price of the first candle of that series, extended out in time. The order block "is just a change in the state of delivery": while price is delivered higher via a run of up-close candles, that run began at the first candle's open; when price trades below that open, the state of delivery changes (from offering buy side to offering sell side, i.e., now seeking sell stops), and the algorithm "remembers" that level, making it sensitive. Mirror for down-close candles: when their opening price is violated upward, it changes to offering buy side and seeks buy stops.
- **How to identify:** A high-probability OB: (1) it sits inside a liquidity pool (e.g., the down-close series inside the sell-stop zone), (2) has an FVG/gap, (3) there was a market structure shift. "Takes liquidity + gap + MSS."
- **How to use:** Bullish: buy limit at the opening price of the down-close candle series, located inside the FVG, plus about 3 pips "or whatever for spread". Bearish: mirror with up-close candles.
- **Example in this video:** NQ 2m: series of down-close candles inside the sell-stop pool below 14,600; their opening price lies inside the FVG = buy limit; price runs to buy stops above highs. NQ 1m: two up-close candles before a drop form one consecutive bearish OB (open extended out), with gap, liquidity taken and MSS: "high probability" bearish OB.
- **Timestamps:** [0:14:32](https://youtu.be/nQfHZ2DEJ8c?t=872) — down-close series = one order block; [0:15:20](https://youtu.be/nQfHZ2DEJ8c?t=920) — open of series inside FVG = buy limit; [0:23:16](https://youtu.be/nQfHZ2DEJ8c?t=1396) — bearish OB with gap, liquidity and MSS; [0:24:06](https://youtu.be/nQfHZ2DEJ8c?t=1446) — OB = change in state of delivery.
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** This is the root of the later "CISD" concept. He says that's all he'll give at the free level. He criticizes others for treating every opposite-colored candle as an OB.

=== [2022 Ep13 — 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 13 (ID tpPtItWqmlg)] — Order block (high-probability bullish order block)  (aliases: bullish order block, down-closed candle, "higher time frame order block")
- **Kind:** definition / rule
- **What ICT says:**
  - In a bullish move, down-close candles should not be violated; they act as support. In a bearish move, up-close candles should not be breached.
  - A high-probability bullish order block = (1) the down-close candle, (2) an imbalance (fair value gap) left when price moves away from / above it, and (3) the underlying narrative that price is likely to go higher to reach buy-side liquidity. "That's it."
  - No engulfing-candle requirement — "forget all that, you don't need that."
  - An hourly down-close candle can be made up of two down-close candles on the 5-minute chart; consecutive down-close candles together are treated as one order block.
  - The "mean threshold" of the order block = the middle of the candle (body/range) — used as the line below which price should not trade once entered.
- **How to identify:** Down-close candle after (or during) a bullish run; price displaces away and leaves an FVG; higher-timeframe narrative points to a BSL target above (e.g. relative equal highs).
- **How to use:** Wait for price to trade back down into the down-close candle, ideally overlapping with the FVG, at a meaningful time (after the 9:30 equities open). Buy there; stop below the order block; target the buy stops above relative equal highs. A lower-timeframe order block + FVG nested inside a higher-timeframe order block gives the entry ("hierarchy higher time frame to a lower time frame").
- **Example in this video:** MNQ, 1-hour chart then 5m / 1m / 30-second, morning session of a Friday in early March 2022. After a run up, a down-close hourly candle formed; price rallied away leaving an imbalance. Relative equal highs above held buy stops, which he marked as roughly 14,110 (red line). After the 9:30 open, price retraced down into the hourly down-close candle (and its gap), in a discount of the low-to-high range (below 50%). On the 5m, price hit the order block, rallied, and traded back down into the gap between one candle's high and another's low, overlapping a lower-timeframe order block = his first long entry. Price went above the next down-close candle's high, retraced into an imbalance without eating through those down-close candles, he bought again; a third, shallower retracement only into an FVG (not back to the order block) was the final add. Price ran to the target above the relative equal highs.
- **Timestamps:** [0:12:51](https://youtu.be/tpPtItWqmlg?t=771) — rally leaves down-close candle; BSL above relative equal highs (~14,110). [0:13:39](https://youtu.be/tpPtItWqmlg?t=819) — rule: in bullish moves down-close candles should not be violated. === [2022 Ep16 — 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 16 (ID EpGQnhjXBq8)] — Fibonacci swing projection on candle bodies ("fulcrum point" targeting)
- **Kind:** entry model / refinement (targeting)
- **What ICT says:**
  - When structure has shown a willingness to go lower — swing low "decisively broken," then a retrace into the FVG — the broken low is a **fulcrum point**. Measure from the swing high down to that low and project the same distance lower ("this is my swing trading model and I'm applying it to targeting").
  - Anchor the Fibonacci on the **highest body** (open or close, doesn't matter which) of the swing high and the **lowest body** (open or close) of the swing low — not the wicks. "The wicks and the tails are all distractions"; this is how the algorithm reads price. He learned it in forex and says it carries over to index futures; it was less pure in the open-outcry era.
  - Using wick high to wick low will not give the measurement — "that's why everybody that uses Fibonacci struggles."
  - Precision only helps when the narrative is already in place (liquidity + discount array); otherwise "a thousand lines on your chart" will hit something.
- **How to identify:** Displacement breaks a swing low; retrace into FVG; measure the bodies of that high-to-low swing.
- **How to use:** Use the projections as targets; choose the one that confluences with the HTF draw (OB / below relative equal lows).
- **Example in this video:** ES 15m, Friday morning: projection levels **4514.50** (first) and **4501.25** (next). 4501.25 sits just below the daily OB at 4504 — and the actual daily low was exactly **4501.25**. Exact Fibonacci extension settings were shown on screen, not spoken.

=== [2022 Ep19 — 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 19 (ID IEa1N0rTtbc)] — Order Block (bearish and bullish)  (aliases: OB, "up close candle", "down close candles", "bookmark")

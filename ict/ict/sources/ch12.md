=== [2022 Ep5 — 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 5 (ID N29ZJ-o31xs)] — Pre-8:30 swing high/low as the stop-hunt reference  (aliases ICT uses: "most significant or obvious swing high and swing low prior to 8:30")

=== [2022 Ep5 — 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 5 (ID N29ZJ-o31xs)] — Daily bias / daily range expansion; daily profiles  (aliases ICT uses: "market profiles", "daily profiles", "morning trend vs afternoon")
- **Kind:** definition / rule
- **What ICT says:** Like the weekly candle, ask whether the daily candle (today's or tomorrow's) is more likely to expand higher or lower — not predicting the close. The day is designed with a morning move, the lunch hour, and an afternoon move. Analogy to forex: the morning's low of the day acts like the "London low" in a buy day, then consolidation, then a New York continuation in the same direction — but here all in NY session time. Afternoon possibilities: reversal of the morning; continuation higher; a measured move (afternoon duplicates the morning move, e.g., 200 points morning + 200 afternoon = 400-point range); or morning consolidation then afternoon trend. Consecutive higher swing lows ("every one higher") show accumulation/underpinning.
- **How to identify:** Classify each day's morning (bullish/bearish/consolidation), what preceded it on prior days, and what the afternoon did.
- **How to use:** If expecting expansion higher, take the afternoon setup in that direction; relative equal highs forming in a bullish context = retail resistance building buy-side liquidity to be run.
- **Example in this video:** ES: bullish context (important low last week on the daily, daily retracing higher; crowd expects a crash and keeps shorting); afternoon ran buy stops into the close.
- **Timestamps:** [0:23:42](https://youtu.be/N29ZJ-o31xs?t=1422) — morning move, lunch, afternoon design; [0:27:46](https://youtu.be/N29ZJ-o31xs?t=1666) — daily candle expansion; London-low analogy; [0:29:20](https://youtu.be/N29ZJ-o31xs?t=1760) — afternoon profiles incl. measured move.

=== [2022 Ep9 — 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 9 (ID iZLXnNiZm_s)] — Don't chase overnight runs; after a big overnight run, avoid the NY AM and trade after lunch
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:**
  - When there's a big overnight move (he cites London, 2:00–5:00 a.m.) before New York, don't chase it at 8:30 just because it went up overnight. Wait for more information.
  - A consolidation typically follows a big run. Not always: sometimes it keeps running and you'll miss it.
  - With a big overnight range, don't expect much in-and-out precision. Wait for a significant move or you'll get chopped up.
  - **Equity index futures only (NQ, YM, ES), not Forex:** if there's a big overnight run, avoid the New York AM session entirely. Wait for the other side of lunch (after 1:00 p.m. NY time) and anticipate a raid on the New York lunch lows or the New York morning-session lows.
  - New York lunch = noon to 1:00 p.m. NY local time.
  - Everyone wakes up wanting to chase. That leads to losing trades and a chase cycle, and in choppy sideways conditions it's "like a blowtorch on your account".
- **How to use:** After the overnight expansion, mark the AM consolidation lows (especially relative equal lows). In the PM, wait for them to be run into a FVG, then a bullish MSS and FVG entry.
- **Example in this video:** NQ, Tue 15 Feb 2022. A huge overnight (London) rally ran into the daily FVG. ICT says he would have missed it and it surprised him. The AM was sloppy and back-and-forth, leaving relative equal lows. Relative equal highs formed before 7:00 a.m. weren't taken. BSL sat around 14,575 and the draw was the upper boundary of the daily FVG. In the PM, NQ dropped, took the sell side and dug into a FVG, with candle bodies respecting the level, then rallied.

=== [2022 Ep10 — 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 10 (ID S9ORTYmXwdE)] — Economic calendar and the 8:30 a.m. news embargo
- **Kind:** rule

=== [2022 Ep10 — 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 10 (ID S9ORTYmXwdE)] — Opening range (open-to-manipulation-extreme, projected through the open) and close-proximity entries
- **Kind:** definition / rule (new framing)
- **What ICT says:**
  - On a bearish day, measure the range from the opening price up to the high (the Judas swing). Subtract that range from the opening price: project it below the open. That projected zone is what he calls the **opening range**.
  - "Every premium array" (every FVG, stop raid and OTE short setup in any approach he teaches, including the paid mentorship) will form between the open and its high, and within that projection below the open.
  - This tells you how much leeway to give the market once it trades below the open while still taking a favorable short. These are **close-proximity entries**: there are many opportunities just below the opening price.
  - If price leaves that zone without you, don't chase. "I don't care how good it looks later in the day."
- **How to identify:** Opening price (intraday, the midnight NY open) and the manipulation high. Distance = high minus open. Zone = open plus or minus that distance.
- **How to use:** Write down intentions before the session: "bearish; look for shorts in the open-to-high range, or just below the open within the projected range." Only take setups inside it.
- **Example in this video:** NQ 15m, 17 Feb 2022. From the midnight opening price, price rallied above it into a FVG (a sell above the open). The open-to-high distance was projected below the open, and a further short formed there at the equities open. He describes the projection as "multiply that by two": from the high, double the high-to-open distance downward.
- **Timestamps:** [0:12:56](https://youtu.be/S9ORTYmXwdE?t=776) — open-to-high range subtracted from the open · [0:13:45](https://youtu.be/S9ORTYmXwdE?t=825) — every premium array forms in that range · [0:14:34](https://youtu.be/S9ORTYmXwdE?t=874) — close-proximity entries below the open; don't chase · [1:00:18](https://youtu.be/S9ORTYmXwdE?t=3618) — write intentions; outside the range, no trade
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** This is ICT's specific 2022-Ep.10 meaning of "opening range". It differs from the RTH 9:30 opening range and gap concepts taught elsewhere.

=== [2022 Ep2 — 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 2 (ID tmeCWULSTHc)] — Weekly bias — "will the weekly candle expand higher or lower?"
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** Before each new trading week (preferably on the weekend), decide whether the next weekly candle is more likely to go higher or lower. You are NOT trying to predict the weekly close; only which side the weekly range expands toward. This sets your initial bias for the week.
- **How to identify:** Look at the weekly chart and the prior weekly candle; consider where price is drawing (an old low/high not yet broken) plus context: seasonality, macro news (Fed rate hikes), earnings season, and the "underlying tone" seen on the daily chart.
- **How to use:** If expecting lower, look only for setups that sell (buy stops run first). The move may happen in the first half of the week or a single day; if you get your setup in that, "you're done" for the week.
- **Example in this video:** NQ March 2022 contract (TradingView symbol NQH2022), weekly chart: bearish expectation for reasons of seasonal tendency to decline at that time of year, Fed expected to raise rates, earnings-season volatility, and a heavy tone; drawing toward an unbroken old weekly/daily low expected to be targeted on Sunday's open into Monday.
- **Timestamps:** [0:12:22](https://youtu.be/tmeCWULSTHc?t=742) — read the next weekly candle before the week; [0:13:11](https://youtu.be/tmeCWULSTHc?t=791) — reasons: seasonality, Fed, earnings; [0:15:32](https://youtu.be/tmeCWULSTHc?t=932) — the weekly expansion may be one day; don't trade every session; [0:16:20](https://youtu.be/tmeCWULSTHc?t=980) — weekly candle sets the initial bias.
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** He did not expect the whole range delivered in one week; what matters is the direction of expansion.

=== [2022 Ep9 — 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 9 (ID iZLXnNiZm_s)] — Power of Three (AMD)  (aliases ICT uses: Power 3, accumulation manipulation distribution)
- **Kind:** definition
- **What ICT says:**
  - Bullish day: the opening price is near the low of the day or session. Price trades lower to make an important low (manipulation), then rallies to create the high and closes near the high.
  - You don't need to predict the close. The aim is to anticipate the fake move at the start.
  - If bullish, the small move lower after the open is what you hunt for a long. If you miss it, try to get long as close to the opening price as possible.
  - Which opening price? He likes 8:30 a.m. ET. Draw it forward in time.
- **How to identify (bullish, checklist he runs at the 8:30 open):** Did price go below the 8:30 opening price? Did it trade into the imbalance? Did it take a short-term low? Did it hit an order block? Was it an optimal trade entry? Each yes adds confluence.
- **How to use:** Buy below the opening price during the manipulation leg, with a target toward the day's draw (e.g. the high end of the daily FVG or relative equal highs).
- **Example in this video:** NQ, Mon 14 Feb 2022. Price was below the 8:30 open, inside the pre-8:30 imbalance, took a short-term low, hit an order block, at OTE: a long. In the afternoon price traded below the opening price again, then rallied and took the relative equal highs toward the upper end of the daily FVG.
- **Timestamps:** [0:02:34](https://youtu.be/iZLXnNiZm_s?t=154) — Power 3 bullish profile · [0:05:46](https://youtu.be/iZLXnNiZm_s?t=346) — hunt the small move lower; use the 8:30 opening price · [0:06:34](https://youtu.be/iZLXnNiZm_s?t=394) — 8:30 confluence checklist

=== [2022 Ep10 — 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 10 (ID S9ORTYmXwdE)] — Daily bias = will the daily range expand above or below the open  (aliases ICT uses: the key to bias)
- **Kind:** definition / rule
- **What ICT says:**
  - The most-requested question over the years is how to find daily bias. He says this is "really all I teach my students", with nothing more secret than this.
  - You only need to know whether the daily range is more likely to expand higher or lower than the opening price. Predicting the close is "more advanced" and not needed to be profitable.
  - Bearish day: open, a rally (small, or even a large wick), down, close near the low. Bullish day: the reverse.
  - Not every candle in a swing conforms. In sustained HTF swings there are few opposite candles. Don't let one counter day knock you out of your bias.
  - Once bias is set (e.g. bearish after a daily MSS and a retrace into a FVG), "any rally or up-close candle should be viewed as a potential shorting candidate".
  - Stay with the bias until the draw (e.g. relative equal highs, BSL) is reached. Then study whether it wants to continue.
- **How to identify:** daily structure (swing points), daily FVG and premium/discount, the draw on liquidity.
- **How to use:** Each day, expect a Power-of-Three candle in the bias direction. Losses on counter days are expected. Keep the bias until the target liquidity is taken.
- **Example in this video:**
  - NQ daily (1992-style OHLC bars, then candles). Bullish sequence:
    - A 3-bar swing low forms. The next day washes out below the open and closes on its high.
    - The following day is expected to rally into the FVG above (bullish bias).
    - Price then retraces below equilibrium of the swing into discount, followed by a series of open-near-low, close-near-high days.
    - It keeps reaching for relative equal highs (BSL). Bias stays bullish until above them.
  - Bearish sequence: the rally into a daily FVG, then open-rally-close-on-low days toward sell-side lows, then a potential key reversal and chop.
  - Current context: NQ traded up to the top of the daily FVG (from Ep. 9), the bias is bearish, and the targets are the lows below.
- **Timestamps:** [0:03:18](https://youtu.be/S9ORTYmXwdE?t=198) — "number one question": daily bias · [0:06:31](https://youtu.be/S9ORTYmXwdE?t=391) — rallies are shorting candidates · [0:17:44](https://youtu.be/S9ORTYmXwdE?t=1064) — expand higher or lower than the open is "the key to bias" · [0:20:57](https://youtu.be/S9ORTYmXwdE?t=1257) — daily bullish Power-3 sequence to relative equal highs
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** Simplifies the "draw on liquidity" framing from Ep. 7 into the open-relative expansion question.

=== [2022 Ep10 — 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 10 (ID S9ORTYmXwdE)] — Power of Three on the daily candle (AMD components, precise)
- **Kind:** definition
- **What ICT says (bearish day):**
  - **Accumulation:** the open and anything above it is where shorts are accumulated (smart money, and ICT and his students, aim to enter there).
  - **Manipulation:** the initial rally above the open, the "sucker play" for breakout traders. It only goes up to go down. This is the Judas swing.
  - **Distribution:** from the low of the day to the close, where the short positions are distributed (closed).
  - The low of the day often forms late. If price has been beaten down all day, around 3:30–3:45 p.m. ET it's probably close to the low. Not always: sometimes there's a sudden continuation.
  - Bullish is mirrored: open near the low, a false move down, a rally, distribution of longs near the close.
- **Timestamps:** [0:12:56](https://youtu.be/S9ORTYmXwdE?t=776) — components defined; open and above = accumulation of shorts · [0:16:09](https://youtu.be/S9ORTYmXwdE?t=969) — manipulation = sucker play; distribution · [0:16:56](https://youtu.be/S9ORTYmXwdE?t=1016) — low of day near 3:30–3:45 p.m.

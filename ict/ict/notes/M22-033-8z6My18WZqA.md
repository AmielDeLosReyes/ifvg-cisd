## Concepts

### Waiting for the key short-term low (not ultra-aggressive in high volatility)
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** He wasn't interested in anything until the 3915.25 short-term low (15m) broke. You could short smaller short-term low breaks "if you want to be ultra aggressive," but with high volatility he wants to "know that I know": he doesn't want to catch a break below a minor short-term low and then see price run relative equal highs above. He was giving price a chance to run those highs first.
- **How to identify:** Pick the short-term low whose break on the 15m clearly confirms the shift out of consolidation.
- **Example in this video:** Consolidation; his prior paper position's final balance stopped out on a run up into a deeper FVG which made the high of day; then the break of 3915.25 around the 10:00 hour.
- **Timestamps:** [0:01:01](https://youtu.be/8z6My18WZqA?t=61) — 3915.25 was the turning point; [0:04:15](https://youtu.be/8z6My18WZqA?t=255) — wanted that low broken; [0:10:37](https://youtu.be/8z6My18WZqA?t=637) — why not ultra aggressive; [0:13:00](https://youtu.be/8z6My18WZqA?t=780) — off air at 10:00 anticipating the low being taken.

### FVG after an old low breaks — not "support turned resistance"
- **Kind:** rule (important distinction)
- **What ICT says:** After a run below an old low, he shorts the retrace into the fair value gap — not a retest of the old low as resistance. "If the fair value gap doesn't exist, I don't trust that level as an old support broken turned resistance." His levels are pre-classified by liquidity logic (where orders are), and the market creates patterns around them. The level shows where liquidity rests; the imbalance is what he keys off.
- **How to identify:** Old low (sell-side) taken with displacement; FVG left in the move; retrace into FVG.
- **Example in this video:** After 3915.25 breaks: FVG (visible on 5m, not 15m) → short; then a further FVG after breaking 3872.25 → another short; price didn't stop at the 3872.25 level on the retrace because it went up into the imbalance above it, then delivered lower to and through 3855.
- **Timestamps:** [0:09:00](https://youtu.be/8z6My18WZqA?t=540) — not break-and-retest; [0:09:48](https://youtu.be/8z6My18WZqA?t=588) — why not stop at 3872.25; [0:11:25](https://youtu.be/8z6My18WZqA?t=685) — levels built on liquidity; confluence.

### Premium requirement vs. "in a hurry" delivery — use the short-term dealing range
- **Kind:** refinement
- **What ICT says:** Measuring the 15m displacement leg, the FVG didn't retrace to a premium — he didn't expect it to, because price was "in a hurry" to get below the 3855 level for the week. Drop to the 1m and use the short-term dealing range (swing high to swing low): there the FVG retrace *was* at equilibrium or above (premium), confluent with the 3915.25 level. "You just don't have the experience to see it yet."
- **How to use:** If the HTF leg FVG is not in premium, check the smaller dealing range on the lower timeframe before rejecting the setup.
- **Timestamps:** [0:05:02](https://youtu.be/8z6My18WZqA?t=302) — "some will say it didn't go to a premium"; [0:13:00](https://youtu.be/8z6My18WZqA?t=780) — in a hurry to 3855; [0:14:36](https://youtu.be/8z6My18WZqA?t=876) — 1m short-term dealing range premium.
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** Refines Ep 25's "hard line" premium rule: premium is relative to the dealing range you measure; under urgency, the lower-timeframe dealing range applies.

## Rules, checklists & numbers stated
- Don't act until the key 15m short-term low breaks when volatility is high.
- Old level + FVG = trade; old level without FVG = no trust (not S/R).
- Levels: 3915.25 (trigger), 3872.25, 3855 (weekly sell-side target); May 12, 2022 low run.
- Premium is measured on the relevant dealing range; on the 1m, FVG at ≥ equilibrium = premium.
- 10:00 AM: expected the algorithm to take the low.
- 1:30 PM: afternoon session begins; last hour can rally into a premium FVG; spool into the close.
- Son's live account: $9,751.66 → $13,451.66 (+$3,700 on the day); not typical.

## Glossary candidates
`Ultra aggressive` — taking the earliest minor short-term structure break as the trigger, versus waiting for the key level.
`Short-term dealing range` — the local swing high–swing low (often on 1m) used to judge premium/discount for the entry.
`Algorithmic spool` — aggressive, one-directional run into the close toward a target.

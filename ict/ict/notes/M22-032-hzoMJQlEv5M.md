## Summary
Review of a choppy index-futures day (likely ES — prices ~3850–3940; instrument and date not stated, probably the day after Ep 31, ~Thu May 19, 2022) that ICT narrated live on Twitter. After a down-close outside day, price fell one handle short of the old daily low (3855) and the session turned into a back-and-forth consolidation day. He explains why: falling short of an old low after a down-close outside day tends to produce chop; in the middle of a range odds are 50/50. His answer is the **consolidation-day 3:00–4:00 PM model**: the market holds an initial range until 3 PM, then the algorithm runs outside it (here above relative equal highs to 3933.25+ to take buy-side), and smart money pairs with those buy stops to drive price back to the midpoint of the day's range. He shorted 3940.75, took four off at 3908.75 (middle of range), and left a runner with a limit at 3855.

## Concepts

### Outside day (down close) → choppy next day if old low isn't reached
- **Kind:** definition / rule
- **What ICT says:** An outside day trades above the previous day's high and below the previous day's low (the whole prior range is inside it); here it closed down. The following day, if price trades down toward an old low but falls short of it (the "double bottom" that feels safe), it tends to be choppy — especially after a down-close outside day.
- **Example in this video:** Old daily low at 3855; day's low 3856 (one handle short), day low area 3872.25; old highs at 3933.25. Price then chopped.
- **Timestamps:** [0:00:54](https://youtu.be/hzoMJQlEv5M?t=54) — outside day defined; [0:01:40](https://youtu.be/hzoMJQlEv5M?t=100) — falling short of old low → choppy; [0:03:17](https://youtu.be/hzoMJQlEv5M?t=197) — levels 3872.25, 3855, 3856.

### Setups that look like they'll form but don't (50/50 in the middle of the range)
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** The market often presents what looks like a setup that never comes to fruition. Price traded up into an FVG, worked lower, created a small FVG, bumped into it — "nothing really in here to work with in terms of the model." Probability is statistically 50/50 when price trades back into the middle of the range — no edge there. The FVG "should have offered more movement lower" but didn't take out the short-term low properly before rallying.
- **Timestamps:** [0:04:14](https://youtu.be/hzoMJQlEv5M?t=254) — no model short; 50/50 in middle of range; [0:05:02](https://youtu.be/hzoMJQlEv5M?t=302) — FVG should have offered more.

### Dealing range (trade ideas framed by the current range)
- **Kind:** definition
- **What ICT says:** Even with a longer-term bias (he still favored the 3855 sell-side), every trade idea must be taken based on its *current dealing range* — e.g., from this high down to that low — and measured.
- **Timestamps:** [0:05:02](https://youtu.be/hzoMJQlEv5M?t=302).

### Consolidation day 3:00–4:00 PM model (run outside range → back to midpoint)
- **Kind:** entry model / rule
- **What ICT says:** On a consolidation day the market creates an initial range and stays in it until 3:00 PM. Between 3:00 and 4:00 PM the algorithm often trades outside the bounds of the day's range, then rams back to the middle of the range. It "used to be the bond close" that started this; now 3 PM to the 4 PM equities closing bell sets the tone for on-close orders. Before 3 PM the rally aims at buy-side liquidity; traders see a breakout and buy, and smart money pairs with them to go short. Mark vertical lines at 3 PM and 4 PM; study the middle of the daily range (midpoint of the day's high and low, e.g., from the ~9:30 low to the high).
- **How to identify:** Range-bound session; relative equal highs (buy-side) above the range from shorts trailing stops; 3–4 PM window.
- **How to use:** After price runs the buy-side in 3–4 PM, look on the 1m for a swing low broken, an FVG, and a retrace back up into it → short; target the midpoint of the day's range (partial), runners to a further level.
- **Example in this video:** Shorts from the prior day trailed stops above the old highs; price bumped them shallowly, then rammed through the relative equal highs to above 3933.25. 1m: swing low broken, FVG, retrace into it, sell-off to mid-range. Short 3940.75, four contracts off at 3908.75 (mid-range), runner with limit at 3855.
- **Timestamps:** [0:05:49](https://youtu.be/hzoMJQlEv5M?t=349) — vertical lines at 3 PM/4 PM; [0:06:39](https://youtu.be/hzoMJQlEv5M?t=399) — middle of the daily range; [0:08:14](https://youtu.be/hzoMJQlEv5M?t=494) — 1m swing low, FVG, retrace; [0:09:07](https://youtu.be/hzoMJQlEv5M?t=547) — fills 3940.75 / 3908.75 / limit 3855; [0:10:43](https://youtu.be/hzoMJQlEv5M?t=643) — consolidation day defined; [0:11:32](https://youtu.be/hzoMJQlEv5M?t=692) — bond close, on-close orders.
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** Choppy days: "wait until three o'clock." Most traders are too afraid to short after a breakout run in the last hour — that's the edge.

### Protective buy stops as a "rushing liquidity wave"
- **Kind:** definition
- **What ICT says:** Buy orders resting above relative equal highs are short sellers' protective stops. When price trades above, those stops become "a rushing liquidity wave" of willing buyers at the market — which smart money sells into. The run also induces breakout longs, building sell-side liquidity below (their stops) for later.
- **Timestamps:** [0:07:26](https://youtu.be/hzoMJQlEv5M?t=446) — run to squeeze shorts and induce longs; [0:16:20](https://youtu.be/hzoMJQlEv5M?t=980) — rushing liquidity wave.

### Gravitation to the midpoint of the daily range
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** On a range-bound day, split the low and high of the day; the midpoint is where price "wants to gravitate back to" after the late-day excursion outside the range.
- **Timestamps:** [0:17:06](https://youtu.be/hzoMJQlEv5M?t=1026).

## Rules, checklists & numbers stated
- Outside day: higher high AND lower low than previous day.
- After a down-close outside day, failing to reach the old low → expect a choppy day.
- Middle of the range = 50/50; avoid.
- Consolidation day: range until 3:00 PM; 3:00–4:00 PM excursion outside the day's range, then return to midpoint.
- Levels: old highs 3933.25; old daily low 3855 (missed by one handle at 3856); session low 3872.25.
- Trade: short 3940.75; 4 off at 3908.75 (mid-range); limit 3855 for the remainder.

## Glossary candidates
`Outside day` — daily range exceeding both the prior day's high and low.
`Consolidation day` — session that holds an initial range until the 3–4 PM excursion.
`Dealing range` — the current high-to-low range used to frame and measure a trade idea.
`Middle of the range (daily midpoint)` — 50% of the day's high-low; target after the late-day excursion.
`Rushing liquidity wave` — protective stops above highs that become market buy orders when triggered.
`Bond close` — the 3:00 PM bond market close that historically started the late-day move.

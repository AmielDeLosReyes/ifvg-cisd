## Concepts

### SMT divergence (Smart Money Technique / Tool)
- **Kind:** definition / example
- **What ICT says:** Using intermarket relationships, not indicators: when one correlated index makes a lower low and the other refuses to, that divergence (he "dubs" it SMT — smart money technique or smart money tool) supports the bullish bias. Used here only as confirmation within an existing bullish bias.
- **How to identify:** Compare ES and NQ swing lows at the same time; one breaks the prior low (e.g., the 10:00 AM low), the other holds it.
- **How to use:** Favors longs in the index that held (here he traded ES, the more "structured" one); confirms the low is unlikely to be taken.
- **Example in this video:** ES 1m: drop to relative equal lows, retrace, then later decline fails to go below the 10:00 AM low; NQ meanwhile made a lower low below its 10:00 AM low → bullish SMT.
- **Timestamps:** [0:03:18](https://youtu.be/QkokVv0owSE?t=198) — ES vs NQ comparison; [0:04:07](https://youtu.be/QkokVv0owSE?t=247) — "this is what I dub SMT."

### Displacement-leg 50% rule invalidates a counter-bias short
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** A rally, break of a short-term low, FVG, price trades up into it and breaks down — "big deal," it doesn't fit: the displacement leg requires the FVG to be above 50% of the leg's high-to-low (premium) for a short. Also bias was bullish, so no interest in shorts.
- **Timestamps:** [0:04:57](https://youtu.be/QkokVv0owSE?t=297).
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** Same premium/discount filter as Ep 25 (sell only above equilibrium of the displacement leg).

### Long setup: sell-stop run → short-term MSS → FVG (1m ES)
- **Kind:** entry model application
- **What ICT says:** At 9:30 the market took a short-term high and declined — no short pattern (no FVG revisited). Price formed relative equal lows; later it ran sell stops, rallied (short-term shift in market structure), and retraced into a fair value gap — the long. A second bullish structure shift (highs taken) left another small FVG, where he added via limit order. Target: liquidity above the highs.
- **Example in this video:** ES 1m, May 16 2022: entries 3994.50 and 3996.25 (first had under two handles of heat, low ~3994.50; second about one handle, caption unclear); added position filled by limit with ~1.75 handles (7 ticks) of heat; full exit at 4022.50 (missed the very high). No NQ trades.

### Close proximity entries
- **Kind:** refinement
- **What ICT says:** If you miss the ideal fill inside the FVG (fumbled order entry, software delays), getting in very close to the FVG without letting price run too far away is acceptable — "I don't think there's anything wrong with it." The point is to enter where the model suggests. In this example the FVG zone gave about eight candles of opportunity to get in inside or near it.
- **Example in this video:** His entry candle only wicked down toward the FVG; his fill was near, not in, the gap; less than ~2 handles of heat.
- **Timestamps:** [0:06:32](https://youtu.be/QkokVv0owSE?t=392) — close proximity entries introduced; [0:07:19](https://youtu.be/QkokVv0owSE?t=439) — missed ideal entry; [0:08:08](https://youtu.be/QkokVv0owSE?t=488) — eight candles of opportunity.

## Rules, checklists & numbers stated
- Bias first: no shorts considered on a bullish-bias day.
- Short FVG must sit above 50% of the displacement leg (premium); longs below 50%.
- SMT: ES held its 10:00 AM low while NQ broke its own → bullish.
- Close-proximity fills near the FVG are acceptable.
- Day before Fed chair speech = low volatility; Fed chair at 2:00 PM and 8:30 retail sales = high volatility, avoid.
- Trade: ES long 3994.50 / 3996.25, add on second FVG, exit 4022.50.

## Glossary candidates
`SMT (smart money technique/tool)` — divergence between correlated markets (ES vs NQ) at swing points, used as confirmation.
`Close proximity entry` — entry near, not exactly inside, the FVG when the ideal fill is missed.
`Heat` — adverse excursion after entry (measured in handles/ticks).
`Demo baller` — ICT's self-label for showcasing paper-trading results.

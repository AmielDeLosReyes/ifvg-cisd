## Summary
Short worked-example episode applying the Episode 2–3 model (liquidity run → intraday market structure shift → FVG entry → target below equilibrium) to two days: ES on Wednesday January 26, 2022 (2m chart) and NQ on Thursday January 27, 2022 (5m chart, then work down). It emphasizes that the rules are specific — if the break lower does not create a fair value gap, there is no trade — and introduces 8:30 AM as the start of "the hunt". It also details what to write in the study journal for each example (time from MSS to FVG retrace, time from entry to target, drawdown/heat).

## Concepts
### Range, Fibonacci equilibrium and target selection  (aliases ICT uses: "equilibrium", "50 level", "discount")
- **Kind:** rule (applied)
- **What ICT says:** Define the range from a chosen low to the high of the run; drop a Fib on it; the 50% is equilibrium. When the run pushes price into premium (expensive), wait for the bearish shift; target a low or an imbalance below equilibrium.
- **How to identify:** Low of the range to high of the range; 50% level.
- **How to use:** Short targets: swing low or imbalance below the 50%; multiple targets can be taken in sequence.
- **Example in this video:** ES March 2022, Wed Jan 26 2022, 2m: range from a marked low to the high; after the run up into premium, a swing low and an imbalance below the 50% serve as targets; price hits the imbalance, bounces, then draws down into the more pronounced low.
- **Timestamps:** [0:00:56](https://youtu.be/L-ReMHiavPM?t=56) — range low to high, Fib, equilibrium; [0:02:35](https://youtu.be/L-ReMHiavPM?t=155) — low or imbalance below 50% as target.
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** none.

### Market Structure Shift requires an FVG to qualify  (aliases ICT uses: "break in market structure", "bearish market structure shift")
- **Kind:** rule / refinement
- **What ICT says:** After the old high is run, wait for a break below a short-term swing low. If that break does NOT create a fair value gap, it doesn't meet the criteria — no trade; wait for the next break. "It's there or it's not"; you can't force it. Waiting forces the market to show its hand.
- **How to identify:** Old high violated (buy stops) → swing low broken by a displacement candle → FVG present between the high and the breaking candle's low.
- **How to use:** Only once the FVG exists, sell when price trades back up into it.
- **Example in this video:** ES 2m Jan 26: first break below the old high's structure had no FVG (skip); price rallied again, then broke down with an MSS and FVG, retraced into it, and sold off into the old low below equilibrium. NQ 5m Jan 27: an initial drop from relative equal highs gave no FVG (no trade); price then ran above an old high (buy-side liquidity), then a swing low was traded through; "soon as this candle trades through it" you have an opportunity; FVG from the high to that candle's low; price rallied back into it (short), broke down, retraced into it again, and reached below equilibrium hitting all targets (lows and the imbalance) and a bit further.
- **Timestamps:** [0:01:44](https://youtu.be/L-ReMHiavPM?t=104) — breaks down but no FVG; [0:06:34](https://youtu.be/L-ReMHiavPM?t=394) — no FVG = doesn't meet criteria; wait; [0:07:22](https://youtu.be/L-ReMHiavPM?t=442) — swing low broken, FVG, short.
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** Consistent with Ep. 3: the opportunity starts as soon as the candle trades through the swing low (no close required).

### 8:30 AM — start of the hunt  (aliases ICT uses: "8:30 in the morning starts the hunt")
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** 8:30 AM (NY time) "starts the hunt" for him: from then he looks for an old/short-term high (or low) to be violated.
- **How to identify:** Previous short-term high/low prior to 8:30.
- **How to use:** After 8:30, watch for the raid on the nearby old high/low, then the MSS + FVG.
- **Example in this video:** ES 2m Jan 26: previous short-term high violated after 8:30, then the swing low break (MSS), FVG retrace short around 4419 covered around 4382 (he rounds "to be not perfect"; about 37 points of opportunity).
- **Timestamps:** [0:02:35](https://youtu.be/L-ReMHiavPM?t=155) — 8:30 starts the hunt; [0:03:23](https://youtu.be/L-ReMHiavPM?t=203) — MSS, FVG short, target old low; [0:04:11](https://youtu.be/L-ReMHiavPM?t=251) — ~4419 to ~4382.
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** none.

### Fair Value Gap entry (ideal entry candle)
- **Kind:** entry model (applied)
- **What ICT says:** The FVG after the MSS is the ideal entry; the target is the discount old low. Using the FVG candle's high as an entry you technically could have been filled, but he wants you to study the total range available in hindsight.
- **How to identify:** As above.
- **How to use:** Short the retrace into the FVG; cover at old low / imbalance in discount.
- **Example in this video:** ES Jan 26 and NQ Jan 27 (above).
- **Timestamps:** [0:04:11](https://youtu.be/L-ReMHiavPM?t=251); [0:04:59](https://youtu.be/L-ReMHiavPM?t=299).
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** none.

### Top-down: start on 5m and work down
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** For the NQ example, the 5-minute chart is "the timeframe you start with and you work down".
- **How to identify:** n/a.
- **How to use:** Identify the range, relative equal highs/old highs on 5m; refine on lower timeframes.
- **Example in this video:** NQ 5m, Thu Jan 27 2022.
- **Timestamps:** [0:05:46](https://youtu.be/L-ReMHiavPM?t=346).
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** Earlier episodes used 15m for liquidity pools; here 5m is the start.

## Rules, checklists & numbers stated
- No FVG after the structure break = no trade.
- Opportunity begins as soon as a candle trades through the short-term swing low (bearish) after buy stops are taken.
- 8:30 AM ET starts the hunt.
- Targets: swing low / imbalance below the 50% of the range; low-hanging fruit first.
- ES example: hypothetical short ~4419, cover ~4382.
- He pays about $4/month for real-time TradingView data (CME).

## Process, risk & psychology
- Use your own charts/feed, not only his examples; annotate your own ([0:00:08](https://youtu.be/L-ReMHiavPM?t=8), [0:03:23](https://youtu.be/L-ReMHiavPM?t=203)).
- Journal fields for each example: how long from the MSS to the retrace into the FVG ("real important"); how long from entry to target; how much heat/drawdown you would have weathered; plus any insightful factors, typed in open space on the chart ([0:04:11](https://youtu.be/L-ReMHiavPM?t=251), [0:04:59](https://youtu.be/L-ReMHiavPM?t=299)).
- Study total range available in hindsight to build the framework; examples share many similarities but are never photocopies ([0:04:59](https://youtu.be/L-ReMHiavPM?t=299)).

## Summary
A short live-funds (thinkorswim) NQ trade recorded while ICT was on vacation, aimed at taking the account to a $50,000 equity mile marker; the account is meant to illustrate a realistic ~20%/month result for a student with a worked-out model. With a bullish daily bias, he waits for a swing high to break (bullish structure), the FVG to complete, and then buys the FVG at the 9:30 open, explaining why a sweep of the short-term low below the gap (sell stops at 13,612) does not negate the setup. He targets the "680" level (an FVG above equilibrium, i.e. premium), uses a mental stop because the open whips stops, and notes the day had a major scheduled event (FOMC, caption unclear) so trading must be done early. Result: $1,255 on two trades; done for the month of March.

## Concepts

### Market structure shift (bullish) + FVG completion
- **Kind:** entry model (applied)
- **What ICT says:** He wants to see the swing high broken — that makes market structure bullish. Then he waits for the candle to close to "complete the imbalance" and marks the low end of the fair value gap.
- **How to identify:** Swing high taken with displacement; the third candle's close defines the FVG.
- **How to use:** Buy when price returns into the FVG.
- **Example in this video:** NQ (live account), morning: swing high broken → bullish; FVG formed; entry in the FVG around the 9:30 open.
- **Timestamps:** [0:00:06](https://youtu.be/tGxuitjtO88?t=6) — wants the swing high broken. [0:01:45](https://youtu.be/tGxuitjtO88?t=105) — waiting for the candle close to complete the imbalance.

### 9:30 open volatility / stop-run below the FVG (narrative over mechanics)
- **Kind:** rule / refinement
- **What ICT says:**
  - At the 9:30 equities open, volatility can overshoot the FVG low; if price comes back up into the FVG he will buy it.
  - Buying blindly at the open is risky — "you don't know how far it's going to whip below that low."
  - Price going below the just-formed short-term low (which is outside and below the FVG) does not negate the FVG, because he uses narrative: traders see the bullish move, buy, and place stops below that swing low; those sell stops (13,612) get taken.
  - Entering long right there is a "high-risk entry."
- **How to identify:** Bullish structure + FVG + a short-term low just below it holding obvious sell stops; the open sweeps them.
- **How to use:** After the sweep, buy as price re-enters the FVG; expect energetic expansion afterwards.
- **Example in this video:** NQ: sell stops at 13,612 were taken at the open, then price re-entered the FVG and he went long.
- **Timestamps:** [0:02:35](https://youtu.be/tGxuitjtO88?t=155) — open can overshoot; don't know how far it whips. [0:03:23](https://youtu.be/tGxuitjtO88?t=203) — narrative; sell stops at 13,612 taken.
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** Refines a strict "FVG invalid if its low is broken" reading — a sweep of the adjacent short-term low can be part of the setup.

### Premium / discount — target an FVG in premium
- **Kind:** rule (applied)
- **What ICT says:** There's an FVG at the "680" level, above equilibrium, so it's in a premium; that's why he targets it ("the 80 level"). About 50 handles away → roughly $1,000+.
- **Example in this video:** NQ target ≈ 13,680 (stated as "680"/"80 level" — full price inferred from the 13,612 level).
- **Timestamps:** [0:05:00](https://youtu.be/tGxuitjtO88?t=300).
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** He believes price goes much higher (daily could reach ~14,000, "a bit of a stretch") but takes the nearer premium FVG because he's on vacation and wants the round equity figure.

### Displacement / expansion after entry
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** After entering at the open he wants price to "expand aggressively," an "energetic run" above the swing high just to the left — no dilly-dallying.
- **Timestamps:** [0:05:00](https://youtu.be/tGxuitjtO88?t=300), [0:05:48](https://youtu.be/tGxuitjtO88?t=348).

### Fair Value Gap as support during the run
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** A small FVG formed in the last three candles up; it doesn't need to be filled or revisited, but if revisited, price should be supported by it — i.e. not trade below the "630" level.
- **Timestamps:** [0:05:48](https://youtu.be/tGxuitjtO88?t=348).

### Entries & stops — mental stop at the open
- **Kind:** rule / caveat
- **What ICT says:** He normally uses a stop loss, but when trading the open stops "can be taken really easily and then reverse." He allows price to go slightly below the low and would close at market. He explicitly does not encourage trading without a stop; he is at the screen ready to close. Once in profit he could put the stop at entry.
- **Timestamps:** [0:04:13](https://youtu.be/tGxuitjtO88?t=253), [0:05:00](https://youtu.be/tGxuitjtO88?t=300), [0:06:45](https://youtu.be/tGxuitjtO88?t=405).
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** Exception for himself at the screen, not a recommendation.

### News drivers — high-impact event day (FOMC, caption unclear)
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** If stopped at break-even he would move to the sidelines and take no more trades that day because of the event (caption reads "F1 see"; likely FOMC). On such days "you can trade in the morning but you got to be done early"; equity traders must be aware of the economic calendar because volatility comes later in the day.
- **Timestamps:** [0:06:45](https://youtu.be/tGxuitjtO88?t=405).

## Rules, checklists & numbers stated
- Bullish only after the swing high breaks; wait for the FVG candle to close.
- At 9:30 ET the open can overshoot the FVG low; buy when price returns into the FVG.
- Sweep of the short-term low below the FVG (sell stops 13,612) does not negate the FVG.
- Target: FVG at "680" in premium (above equilibrium); ~50 handles ≈ $1,000+.
- Small FVG from the last three up-candles should hold (no trade below "630").
- Drawdown on the trade: ~3 handles (~$60).
- On a major-event day (FOMC per likely caption): trade the morning only, be done early.
- Result: $1,255 on two trades, ~$15 commissions; no more trades in March. Account goal ~20% per month; target equity $50,000.

## Glossary candidates
`Complete the imbalance` — wait for the third candle to close so the FVG is fully defined.
`Handle` — one full index point (e.g. NQ 13,630 → 13,680 = 50 handles).
`High-risk entry` — entering at the open before the stop-run below has resolved.

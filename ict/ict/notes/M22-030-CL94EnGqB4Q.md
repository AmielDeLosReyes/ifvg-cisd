## Summary
Tuesday May 17, 2022 (Fed chair speaking at 2:00 PM). On NQ, ICT works bottom-up from the 1‑minute chart to the daily to show an afternoon-session long that finally delivered price to the 12,553.25 relative equal highs he had been pointing to. Ahead of 2 PM, a Judas run fell one tick short of the morning high, then price dropped below the New York lunch lows (relative equal lows) into a 1m imbalance — a manipulation that shook out longs — before an order block held and the 3:00 PM algorithmic run took the buy-side. Key teachings: on a bullish day that consolidates at lunch, wait for the lunch lows to be run, then expect continuation; the model gave no short on the afternoon sell-off — absence of a setup is itself information.

## Concepts

### PM session continuation: lunch lows get swept
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** When there is an afternoon continuation to the upside, the lows made during the New York lunch hour (12:00–1:00) will often be swept first. "If you're bullish and it consolidates in lunch, find the lunch lows, wait for it to run that out, and then they'll resume."
- **How to identify:** Bullish daily bias; lunch-hour range with relative equal lows; afternoon drop below them (often into an imbalance below).
- **How to use:** Look for longs after the lunch lows are run; target the HTF draw (here 12,553.25).
- **Example in this video:** NQ 1m: lunch (noon–1 PM) relative equal lows run in the afternoon, price digs into an imbalance below them, then rallies to 12,553.25.
- **Timestamps:** [0:00:53](https://youtu.be/CL94EnGqB4Q?t=53) — lunch lows swept on afternoon continuation; [0:11:23](https://youtu.be/CL94EnGqB4Q?t=683) — restated rule.

### Judas swing ahead of 2:00 PM Fed speak (falls one tick short)
- **Kind:** example only
- **What ICT says:** Ahead of the 2:00 PM Fed chair "jawboning," a Judas run cleared relative equal highs but stopped at 12,535.50 vs. the morning high 12,535.75 — one tick short — then broke lower. No model short formed there.
- **Timestamps:** [0:00:00](https://youtu.be/CL94EnGqB4Q?t=0); [0:00:53](https://youtu.be/CL94EnGqB4Q?t=53).

### Stop-run manipulation narrative (longs raked, shorts squeezed)
- **Kind:** definition / example
- **What ICT says:** The run up triggered buy stops / breakout and bull-flag buyers, who were then taken into drawdown and stopped out (or squeezed out) below the relative equal lows; morning longs who trailed stops below the lunch lows were also taken out — so they can't profit on the real move to 12,553.25. Then shorts (e.g., from ~12,530) who feel safe get squeezed as price rips higher. The FVG overhead that looks like "resistance" isn't — "it's going to lift off from here."
- **Timestamps:** [0:02:31](https://youtu.be/CL94EnGqB4Q?t=151) — buy stops triggered, longs squeezed out; [0:04:08](https://youtu.be/CL94EnGqB4Q?t=248) — not resistance; shorts' internal dialogue.

### Order block support + 3:00 PM algorithmic spool to liquidity
- **Kind:** rule / example
- **What ICT says:** After the drop, price went down to an order block and rallied; it traded back into the order block multiple times. He allowed for a fill in the FVG but it didn't get there. At 3:00 PM "the algorithms will start to spool price in the direction of the liquidity" — 12,553.25 — which it hammered. Sequence: OB → rally → OB → higher → consolidate right below the target → pump through.
- **Example in this video:** NQ 1m: long entry ~12,417.75 (just below the 20 level; poor entry — missed the imbalance fill); stop below the relative equal lows; exit at/above 12,553.25. ~$5,000 hypothetical.
- **Timestamps:** [0:04:55](https://youtu.be/CL94EnGqB4Q?t=295) — OB, 3 PM spool; [0:05:42](https://youtu.be/CL94EnGqB4Q?t=342) — entry 12,417.75, missed FVG; delivery sequence.

### No setup = market not likely to deliver that way
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** On the 5m, the afternoon sell-off produced no model setup (even without running buy-side). When the market is "in sync," these algorithmic principles will not appear in moves that are not likely to deliver — not always, but often; the market can sell off without giving a setup and then create the real move the other way where the setups do form.
- **Timestamps:** [0:07:19](https://youtu.be/CL94EnGqB4Q?t=439); [0:08:08](https://youtu.be/CL94EnGqB4Q?t=488).

### HTF confirmation: 15m imbalance / OTE and daily relative equal highs
- **Kind:** example only
- **What ICT says:** 15m: price traded down into an imbalance — an optimal trade entry area — before rallying for the liquidity at 12,553.25. Daily: the run into the relative equal highs was the bias he had stated. Next upside reference: the 12,624.00 high (area "sloppy" beyond).
- **Timestamps:** [0:08:57](https://youtu.be/CL94EnGqB4Q?t=537) — 15m imbalance, OTE; [0:09:46](https://youtu.be/CL94EnGqB4Q?t=586) — next target 12,624; [0:10:37](https://youtu.be/CL94EnGqB4Q?t=637) — daily view.

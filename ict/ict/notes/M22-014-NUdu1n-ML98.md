## Summary
A short live (demo/paper) execution on Micro Nasdaq futures, recorded at a student's request ("James") to show the mentorship model in action. After a bullish market structure shift, ICT places a limit buy in the fair value gap left by the displacement, targeting buy-side liquidity above 14,160 and the fill of the gap from Friday's close. He narrates what he watches while in the trade (candles, not P&L), expects a bull-flag-like reaccumulation at a small FVG, uses ES as confirmation, takes half off near the first target, and lets a limit order at 14,220 fill. Useful as a compact template for entry, partials, and break-even management.

## Concepts

### 2022 Mentorship model (MSS → FVG limit entry → BSL target)
- **Kind:** entry model (applied)
- **What ICT says:** Market structure shift to the upside, then wait for price to drop back into the fair value gap; enter with a limit order there; aim for a run above the buy-side level.
- **How to identify:** Bullish MSS (short-term high taken) with displacement leaving an FVG; an obvious BSL level above; an opening gap above to fill.
- **How to use:** Limit buy in the FVG; first target at/above the BSL level; scale out; final target at the gap-fill/limit level.
- **Example in this video:** Micro Nasdaq (MNQ), morning session (the session after Friday's close — likely a Monday; date not stated). Bullish MSS; long 12 micros on a limit order inside the FVG — the fill was at the low candle. Targets: run above 14,160 (BSL), initial interest 14,180, 14,220 "possible today". Price ran above the short-term high made prior to 10:00 a.m. and made a new equity-session high.
- **Timestamps:** [0:00:01](https://youtu.be/NUdu1n-ML98?t=1) — MSS up, gap fill from Friday's close, long 12 micros, targets 14,160/14,180/14,220. [0:00:48](https://youtu.be/NUdu1n-ML98?t=48) — limit entry; FVG closed. [0:03:56](https://youtu.be/NUdu1n-ML98?t=236) — final limit fill; entry was at the low candle.
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** none.

### Opening gap fill (gap from Friday's close)
- **Kind:** example only
- **What ICT says:** He looks for a "gap fill from Friday's close" as the draw; when price got very close to closing that gap he took partial profits.
- **Example in this video:** MNQ — took 6 contracts off as price neared the closure of the gap; 14,220 seen as the full fill/objective.
- **Timestamps:** [0:00:01](https://youtu.be/NUdu1n-ML98?t=1), [0:03:09](https://youtu.be/NUdu1n-ML98?t=189).
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** Exact gap boundaries not stated in the transcript.

### Fair Value Gap as reaccumulation zone (bull-flag behaviour)
- **Kind:** refinement
- **What ICT says:** While long, he watches whether price keeps driving toward and through a small FVG above (~14,122–14,138). Price may consolidate in that area and "reaccumulate for new longs", like a pause / bull flag, roughly 14,120–14,140.
- **How to use:** Consolidation at an FVG during an expansion is not a reason to exit; expect continuation.
- **Example in this video:** MNQ consolidated around the FVG level "reaching into an order block" before continuing up.
- **Timestamps:** [0:00:48](https://youtu.be/NUdu1n-ML98?t=48) — small FVG ~14,122–14,138. [0:01:34](https://youtu.be/NUdu1n-ML98?t=94) — bull-flag-style reaccumulation 14,120–14,140.

### Buy-side liquidity vs retail "resistance"
- **Kind:** rule (applied)
- **What ICT says:** Buy-side liquidity sits around 14,160. Price will often consolidate there with a small retracement that gets people thinking it's time to short; retail sees 14,160 as resistance because price fell from there before. He expects it to "bust through the door" and run to 14,180.
- **Timestamps:** [0:02:22](https://youtu.be/NUdu1n-ML98?t=142).

### Intermarket confirmation (ES with NQ)

### Targets & partials / trade management
- **Kind:** rule (applied)
- **What ICT says:** Plan: take 6 of 12 contracts off above 14,180 "to fund the position," roll the stop to break-even, then leave a limit order at 14,220 for the rest. While in the trade, watch candles (expansion), not the P&L number.
- **Example in this video:** He took 6 off slightly early as price got near the gap closure ("change my mind and close it here"); the remaining limit order then filled.
- **Timestamps:** [0:00:48](https://youtu.be/NUdu1n-ML98?t=48) — "not watching the number underneath that profit". [0:02:22](https://youtu.be/NUdu1n-ML98?t=142) — half off, stops to even, limit at 14,220.

## Rules, checklists & numbers stated
- Entry: limit order in the FVG after a bullish MSS.
- 12 MNQ micros; half (6) off at first objective; stop to break-even; runner via limit order.
- Levels: small FVG ~14,122–14,138; BSL ~14,160; first target 14,180; final 14,220.
- Watch the short-term high formed before 10:00 a.m. ET being run.

## Process, risk & psychology
- Watch price action/expansion, not the open P&L. [0:00:48](https://youtu.be/NUdu1n-ML98?t=48)
- Taking partials "funds the position," then moving to break-even removes risk. [0:02:22](https://youtu.be/NUdu1n-ML98?t=142)

## Glossary candidates
`Fund the position` — take partial profit so the remaining contracts are paid for, then move stop to break-even.
`Gap fill from Friday's close` — price returning to close the opening gap between Friday's close and the new session open.

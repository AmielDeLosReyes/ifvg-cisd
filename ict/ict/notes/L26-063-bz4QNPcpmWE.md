## Concepts

### Probing a low-probability RTH opening range: how far can it go?  (aliases ICT uses: probing the low probability opening range, how far can it drop)
- **Kind:** rule / new framing
- **What ICT says:**
  - The prior day's RTH opening range (Monday's) was a low-probability one. His question was "how far can it go [down]?" - so he looked for the limit of the probe, not the gap's upper volume bound.
  - A viewer pointed to a volume imbalance at the top of the daily candle (close 9:31.25-ish vs open 37.75, caption values); he replies price was already below it, so "it's not warranted to concern ourselves with the upper volume bounds"; only the lower volume imbalance mattered for how far it could go.
  - Monday's open: price opened at 9:30, went up and hit the lowest octant of the opening range gap and "that was it"; it went lower.
  - He says viewers who did not listen to yesterday's lecture assumed he mis-annotated.
- **How to identify:** an RTH opening range gap where price opens and fails (rejects) at a low-octant level, signalling that the range will not be traded fully; then identify the lower key level (daily volume imbalance, low end).
- **How to use:** define the probe limit using the higher-timeframe key level (here the low end of a daily volume imbalance); then use lower-timeframe lows near it to anchor entries; if the probe low gets taken, expect a rally back toward the opening range gap CE and beyond.
- **Example in this video:** NQ Monday 7/13: 9:30 open, price rallies to the lowest octant of Monday's RTH opening range gap and fails; sells to the lower volume imbalance on the RTH session; electronic hours (after 4:15 p.m. ET) take it lower, take out a low, then MSS.

### Choose the first low dropping from 9:30 that sits inside a higher-time-frame key level  (aliases ICT uses: higher low for the day or session, relative equal lows inside the daily volume imbalance, key level agreement)
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:**
  - Go back through old data to the left and determine where a higher low may form for the day or the session; the thing sought is "some higher time frame key level" - weekly or, predominantly, daily: "unless you have a daily chart perspective, you don't have the highest degree of probability."
  - On the 1-minute chart there was a low, relative equal lows and a swing low in the area; he picked the relative equal lows because they were the first low dropping in from where price was at 9:30 and because they sit inside the low end of the daily chart's volume imbalance.
  - He likes them "because I already knew that the volume imbalance agreed with this."
  - Result: the RTH low of the day was there; on the electronic-trading-hours chart price swept slightly below once more and came back; "Model 2022, sells off, goes just below that old low."
  - The low he marked came in at about 29,325.75, "one tick away" from hitting the stated level (caption values, level not further specified).
- **How to identify:** (1) mark the 9:30 candle; (2) scan to the left on the 1m/5m for the first low reached below 9:30 that lies within a daily/weekly array; (3) check relative equal lows or a swing low inside the array.
- **How to use:** anchor longs near that low; stop beyond it; the target is the retracement into the opening range gap (CE and upper half) and the NWOG.
- **Example in this video:** NQ Monday: RTH low (relative equal lows) inside the daily volume imbalance low end; ETH re-sweep just below it; reversal.
- **Timestamps:** [0:03:19](https://youtu.be/bz4QNPcpmWE?t=199) — going left through old price action; [0:04:54](https://youtu.be/bz4QNPcpmWE?t=294) — higher time frame key level, daily; [0:05:42](https://youtu.be/bz4QNPcpmWE?t=342) — first one dropping in from 9:30; [0:07:18](https://youtu.be/bz4QNPcpmWE?t=438) — ETH sweep below, Model 2022
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** He jokes that volume profile, Elliott waves and Gann did not give the low of the day. Provides the HTF-first rule that he repeats ad nauseam.

### Overnight sequence to the London buy: breaker + FVG "unicorn"  (aliases ICT uses: breaker, unicorn, order block, change in the state of delivery, midnight candle)
- **Kind:** entry model / example
- **What ICT says:**
  - After the Asia peak price sold off again, through the volume imbalance (yellow), swept the low, then rallied back through that volume imbalance; the midnight candle and its open are marked.
  - London: price trades lower into a breaker (a low, a high, a lower low = the breaker pattern: "no volume profile, no footprint required") that also has a fair value gap at the same location, which he calls a "unicorn... super strong."
  - He also describes an earlier retracement into an order block with the change in the state of delivery at the open (draws it); price rallies away, comes back into the breaker; creates another small FVG; expects higher prices; relative equal highs above.
  - A scalper could take the first high and be content; otherwise look higher in the direction discussed the day before.
- **How to identify:** low -> high -> lower low sequence forming a breaker; an FVG overlapping at the same price = unicorn.
- **How to use:** buy the retracement into the breaker/FVG after the sweep; target relative equal highs, then the opening range gap.
- **Example in this video:** NQ 1m, London session on 7/14: sweep of the overnight low, rally, retrace into an order block, drop to a breaker with an FVG at the same location, rally away, small FVG, relative equal highs.

## Rules, checklists & numbers stated
- Find how far a probe can go using a higher-timeframe key level (daily volume imbalance low end), not the upper volume bound.
- Go back to the left on the 1m/5m to find the first low below the 9:30 candle that sits inside the HTF array; relative equal lows or swing low.
- The daily chart gives the highest probability; weekly can serve too.
- Price low marked about 29,325.75 (one tick away from the key level, caption); RTH ORG CE about 29,881.5 (caption).
- London breaker + FVG at the same location = unicorn.
- NWOG not fully closed = incomplete, expect further business above.
- No positions through CPI/PPI/NFP.

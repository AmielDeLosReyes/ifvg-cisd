## Concepts

### Relative Equal Lows (in crude oil)
- **Kind:** example only
- **What ICT says:** Crude oil had previous swing lows; when price creates another low near the same level, that becomes a magnet and target. ICT had shared this observation on X the prior day and was aiming for price to drop below the lower of those equal lows.
- **How to identify:** Mark prior swing lows on the chart; when a new low is near them within a small range, it qualifies.
- **How to use:** Target the lower of the two equal lows for a down move; use price respecting or breaking below this level as a sell signal.

### Inversion Fair Value Gap (iFVG) on multi-timeframe setup
- **Kind:** example only
- **What ICT says:** On the 15m chart, crude oil had a buy-side imbalance / sell-side inefficiency (BISI) that, once breached on the downside, became an inversion FVG (would act as resistance on rallies). On the 1m chart, same inefficiency was visible and served as entry confirmation.
- **How to identify:** On 15m: identify the gap, mark it. Drop to 1m and observe price trading into it; watch where body respects the high/low and consequent encroachment.
- **How to use:** Use multi-timeframe to confirm: if 15m shows iFVG forming, drop to 1m for precision entry point. Stop loss placed just above the iFVG high.
- **Example in this video:** Crude oil. 15m showed a BISI → iFVG post-gap-down. On 1m, price traded into the iFVG high, created a whipsaw (bodies touching high, then reversing). Entry was on the dip back into that iFVG; stop placed above the high; target was the relative equal low below.

### Capitulation & Market Mania
- **Kind:** refinement / psychology
- **What ICT says:** When markets become parabolic and everyone is excited (mania), the top is near. Once capitulation occurs (final exhaustion dump), the move is over. Gold and silver both showed this pattern; Bitcoin also. When ICT tells you to stay away, it is because he recognizes the mania phase and knows the capitulation dump is coming. Retail traders, lacking experience, see parabolic moves and assume they continue; they do not.
- **How to identify:** Parabolic run + mania in the news/social media = top forming. Sudden sharp dump after a run = capitulation.
- **How to use:** Do not chase parabolic moves. When ICT says "stay away," he is protecting your capital. After capitulation, do not expect higher prices immediately; wait for structure to reset.
- **Example in this video:** Gold and silver had parabolic runs; ICT warned to stay away; they capitulated lower (like the "Hunt Brothers" manipulation of 1980). Bitcoin aimed for $1M, peaked at $127k, then reversed.
- **Timestamps:** [0:06:24](https://youtu.be/dwMgP2zkH0w?t=384) — capitulation and mania teaching · [0:07:11](https://youtu.be/dwMgP2zkH0w?t=431) — "Hunt brothers" reference to 1980 collapse

### DXY (US Dollar Index) as Market Driver
- **Kind:** refinement
- **What ICT says:** DXY (Dollar Index) is stuck in a range on the daily chart; when DXY does not move, USD pairs do not move. Therefore, trading EURUSD, GBPUSD, etc., will be frustrating and choppy. However, exotic pairs (non-USD pairs) will have movement even when DXY is stuck. The correlation is critical to recognize.
- **How to identify:** Check daily DXY; if it is in a range with no breakout, expect USD pairs to be choppy.
- **How to use:** When DXY is stuck, avoid USD pairs; trade exotics (EURJPY, AUDJPY, GBPJPY, etc.) instead. Wait for DXY to break range before re-engaging USD pairs.
- **Example in this video:** DXY stuck = EURUSD, GBPUSD, etc., not moving. ICT acknowledges this and pivots to non-USD pairs.
- **Timestamps:** [0:04:49](https://youtu.be/dwMgP2zkH0w?t=289) — DXY daily review · [0:05:37](https://youtu.be/dwMgP2zkH0w?t=337) — DXY stuck explanation for pairs
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** Exotic pairs still move even when USD pairs are stuck.

- **What ICT says:** Bitcoin had a SIBI (in this context, a fair value gap); price is revisiting the low and testing the consequent encroachment. ## Glossary candidates
`Capitulation` — a sudden sharp dump in price after a parabolic run, marking the exhaustion of the move; signals weakness and often a reversal.
`Mania phase` — the final stages of a directional move where parabolic price action and herd excitement peak; precedes capitulation.
`Exotic pairs` — forex pairs that do not include the US dollar (e.g., EURJPY, GBPJPY); these can move even when DXY is stuck.
`SIBI (BISI/SIBI context)` — buy-side imbalance / sell-side inefficiency; a fair value gap that can become an iFVG if price approaches from opposite direction.

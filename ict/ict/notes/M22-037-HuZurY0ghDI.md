## Summary
Thursday June 2, 2022, the evening before NFP; E-mini S&P (ES) June 2022. ICT defines a **breaker** and shows a daily FVG/breaker confluence where price filled the gap and began rallying, then gives a simple daily-bias rule: after a swing low, if each day is bullish, bias stays bullish until the target high is taken; a 1–2 day retracement into an FVG afterward is logical. For NFP Friday he gives a conditional expectation (above the daily FVG at 8:30 → expect an attempt at the short-term high) but says don't trade it — study which side of liquidity it reaches for. He reads the day as intraday power of three into a daily FVG low, shows a "spotter" tweet (5m FVG above price at 11:37 AM, hit within ~20 minutes), and demonstrates how to annotate a journal chart positively, as if you foresaw it.

## Concepts

### Breaker (definition) + daily FVG confluence
- **Kind:** definition
- **What ICT says:** A breaker (bullish) forms from a low, a high, then a lower low — find the high in between and extend it out in time; when price trades back down into that area, it's the breaker. Here it overlapped a daily fair value gap; price came down, filled the FVG, and began to rally.
- **How to identify:** Low → high → lower low (sell-side taken) → price rallies above; the in-between high's zone becomes support on retest.
- **Example in this video:** ES daily: price worked down into the FVG/breaker zone, filled the FVG, tested it again and rallied; next buy-side = short-term high above; with acceleration, relative equal highs and an FVG higher up.
- **Timestamps:** [0:00:02](https://youtu.be/HuZurY0ghDI?t=2) — breaker defined; FVG fill; [0:00:48](https://youtu.be/HuZurY0ghDI?t=48) — next targets.

### Daily bias "meat on the bone" — consecutive bullish days until the high is taken
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** When the market moves up from a swing low, it's easy to assume it will come back up to the prior high. If each day is bullish (with maybe one exception), the bias is expected to stay bullish until that high is taken. After it's taken, a day or two of retracement is logical (here back into the FVG it created), then it tests and rallies again.
- **Timestamps:** [0:00:48](https://youtu.be/HuZurY0ghDI?t=48); [0:01:35](https://youtu.be/HuZurY0ghDI?t=95).

### NFP Friday — conditional expectation and why not to trade it
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** Do not speculate on non-farm payroll Friday — it can be very volatile, choppy, or a dud; students lack the experience to weather it. Instead study it: determine what side of liquidity it will reach for before the 8:30 release and watch the 1m/5m when volatility hits. His bias expectation: the short-term high could be run overnight; if not, and at 8:30 price is above the daily FVG's high (the relevant candle's low), expect an attempt toward that high (not necessarily through it).
- **Timestamps:** [0:01:35](https://youtu.be/HuZurY0ghDI?t=95) — don't trade NFP; [0:03:11](https://youtu.be/HuZurY0ghDI?t=191) — 8:30 conditional; [0:03:58](https://youtu.be/HuZurY0ghDI?t=238) — study liquidity sides around the release.

### Stop trading by Wednesday's NY session in NFP weeks
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** He teaches students to stop trading by the New York session on Wednesday: if you haven't "bagged anything" from Sunday's weekly open until Wednesday's NY session begins, don't trade; if you've profited, stop for the week and only observe. Thursday and Friday of NFP weeks are choppy, sporadic, come back against you and "lose a lot of their precision" (not always, most of the time). Avoid arenas where your proven walk-forward results are diminished; it forges discipline and patience.
- **Example in this video:** He forced himself to short on Thursday (covered as price dipped below a short-term low) specifically to show his precision drops on such days.
- **Timestamps:** [0:04:47](https://youtu.be/HuZurY0ghDI?t=287) — stop by Wednesday NY session; [0:05:34](https://youtu.be/HuZurY0ghDI?t=334) — Thu/Fri lose precision; [0:10:18](https://youtu.be/HuZurY0ghDI?t=618) — avoid built-in disadvantages; [0:11:08](https://youtu.be/HuZurY0ghDI?t=668) — Sunday open to Wednesday NY.

### HTF levels transposed to lower timeframes (not S/R)
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** Lines on the hourly are not drawn as hourly support/resistance — they're the daily FVG boundaries (two blue lines) and the daily short-term high (red line), transposed down. Losing that context makes LTF price look like noise.
- **Example in this video:** ES 1h: Wednesday hit the daily FVG low, consolidated, dropped to it again, then a mad dash took the short-term high (bullish MSS) and relative equal highs; next draw = buy-side above; a small FVG below might be revisited first.
- **Timestamps:** [0:06:21](https://youtu.be/HuZurY0ghDI?t=381); [0:07:07](https://youtu.be/HuZurY0ghDI?t=427).

### Power of Three on the intraday chart (not noise)
- **Kind:** rule / example
- **What ICT says:** The day opened (NY midnight opening price), consolidated, rallied, dropped into the daily FVG low (below the midnight open — the manipulation), bounced, took the short-term high, and closed near the high: accumulation, manipulation, distribution. Easy to see on a daily candle; on 1m/5m people call it noise, but it's the same thing on a smaller interval.
- **Timestamps:** [0:07:54](https://youtu.be/HuZurY0ghDI?t=474) — dropping below the NY midnight open; [0:08:42](https://youtu.be/HuZurY0ghDI?t=522) — power of three; [0:09:29](https://youtu.be/HuZurY0ghDI?t=569) — not noise.

### Journaling chart template (2m example) with timing observations
- **Kind:** rule (study method)
- **What ICT says:** Mark up the chart after the fact: order block (the down-close candle before the up move), consolidation, drop into the FVG, then the delivery to the target. Record observations — e.g., price started delivering ~6 minutes (3 × 2m candles) after hitting the FVG and took roughly 20 minutes to reach the target. Phrase every annotation as if you saw it beforehand (self-talk) to build pseudo-experience; keep it factual and positive — never "I wish I'd seen it" or "I'm never going to get this." Review each weekend (previous week/month). Weeks to months; "certainly by the first year." "You will do this or you will fail."
- **Timestamps:** [0:17:33](https://youtu.be/HuZurY0ghDI?t=1053) — arrows are illustration, not trades; OB + FVG; [0:18:20](https://youtu.be/HuZurY0ghDI?t=1100) — 6 min to start, ~20 min to deliver; [0:19:54](https://youtu.be/HuZurY0ghDI?t=1194) — self-talk; [0:20:41](https://youtu.be/HuZurY0ghDI?t=1241) — never negative.
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** Repeats and extends Ep 27's backtesting-annotation method with timing measurements.

## Rules, checklists & numbers stated
- Breaker: low → high → lower low; the in-between high extended in time is the breaker.
- Daily bias stays bullish while each day is bullish, until the target high is taken; then expect a 1–2 day retracement into the FVG.
- NFP Friday: don't trade; if price is above the daily FVG high at 8:30 and the short-term high is untaken, expect an attempt at it.
- NFP weeks: stop trading by the Wednesday NY session (window = Sunday weekly open → Wednesday NY open).
- HTF levels are transposed down, not treated as LTF S/R.
- Spotter tweet 11:37 AM ET June 2, 2022: 5m FVG above price; delivery began ~6 min after the FVG tap, completed in ~20 min.
- Delivery windows: before 12:00 or PM session ~1:00–1:30.

## Glossary candidates
`Breaker` — zone at the high between a low and a lower low (bullish case), extended in time and used as support when revisited.
`Spotter` — ICT's role of pointing to a level to study, not signalling a trade.
`Unfinished business` — an untaken old high/low the market is expected to return to.
`Precision drop` — reduced reliability of setups on Thu/Fri of NFP weeks and similar event days.

# L26-057 — Midnight ET Principles In Relationship To PreMarket Session
ID: KHkUPAsdyfk · URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KHkUPAsdyfk · Duration: 8:01
Content type: mixed (short lecture on the midnight open + same-day market review on NQ, a Friday)
Teaching density: medium (a few transferable rules: midnight open as fulcrum, London lows into 7:00 then a run to a daily draw, where to take profit, OB needs no close)

## Summary
On a Friday, ICT reviews NQ with the New York midnight open and the 7:00-9:00 a.m. ET "pre-market session." A daily relative-equal-low draw on liquidity had been telegraphed all week. Price opened at midnight, made relative equal highs through Frankfurt/London, dropped near 4:40 a.m., retraced into an inefficiency, then accelerated at 7:00 a.m. through the daily relative equal lows, followed by a "massive short covering" rally back up that took the relative equal highs but fell short of the midnight open. The lesson: after London forms relative equal lows and they are retaken right before 7:00, do not expect a return to the midnight open; target the relative equal highs and take profit there. He also restates that an order block does not need a close through its open to be valid, and reflects that he called each day's draws all week on NQ only.

## Concepts

### Midnight open as fulcrum / bookend of the daily range expansion  (aliases ICT uses: midnight Eastern open, New York local midnight, fulcrum point, Power of Three)
- **Kind:** definition / rule
- **What ICT says:**
  - Midnight ET (New York local) is one of the several openings he teaches.
  - The midnight open price "can act as a fulcrum point," a bookend or beginning of the daily range expansion.
  - Bearish template (normal Power of Three): open at midnight, go above the midnight open, then drop lower and go lower for the day, closing somewhere off the low.
  - In this day's example: after the midnight open, price traded through Frankfurt and London forming relative equal highs; the drop did not reach the daily objective until after 7:00.
- **How to identify:** mark the 00:00 ET open on the chart; watch whether price runs above it (bearish) before the main delivery.
- **How to use:** use the midnight open as the reference for expected daily direction and as a possible upper target on a retracement - but see the next concept: a retracement to it is not necessary.
- **Example in this video:** NQ 5m (for the panoramic view), Friday: midnight open at the start; relative equal highs formed in the Frankfurt/London window; the highs created are "significantly lower than the opening price."
- **Timestamps:** [0:01:39](https://youtu.be/KHkUPAsdyfk?t=99) — midnight open as fulcrum; [0:02:28](https://youtu.be/KHkUPAsdyfk?t=148) — bearish power-of-three template and this day
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** He adds that the midnight open is only one of several openings he teaches (others not detailed here).

### Pre-market session 7:00-9:00 ET (informal start at 6:30)  (aliases ICT uses: pre-market session, 7:00 a.m. acceleration)
- **Kind:** rule / time window
- **What ICT says:**
  - Officially the pre-market session runs 7:00 a.m. to roughly 9:00 a.m. ET; it can technically start around 6:30, and if awake he spends 6:30-7:00 "getting a read."
  - "The fact that we're entering 7:00 a.m. - this is when it accelerates": price ran down through the daily relative equal low shortly after 7:00.
  - London killzone had only 20 minutes left when price dropped around 4:40 a.m. (i.e., London ends at 5:00 a.m.).
- **How to use:** expect acceleration at 7:00 toward the high-timeframe draw; use 6:30-7:00 for preparation.
- **Example in this video:** NQ, Friday: about 4:40 a.m. ET drop; retrace up into an inefficiency; 7:00 a.m. acceleration down through the daily relative equal lows.
- **Timestamps:** [0:00:00](https://youtu.be/KHkUPAsdyfk?t=0) — 7:00 start; [0:00:47](https://youtu.be/KHkUPAsdyfk?t=47) — 7:00 to 9:00; [0:03:15](https://youtu.be/KHkUPAsdyfk?t=195) — accelerates at 7:00
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** Matches earlier 7:00-9:00 pre-market framing (see L26-002 for 7:00-8:30 range).

### High-timeframe draw on liquidity: daily relative equal lows with a high-probability move  (aliases ICT uses: unrealized objective, daily relative equal low, hard high time frame draw)
- **Kind:** rule / example
- **What ICT says:** A "hard high time frame draw on liquidity" (here the daily relative equal lows on Nasdaq) is "very high probability" to be reached; he had explained for several days why lower prices were likely, and it "happened again." A minor sell-side pool lay just above the daily draw (price quoted in captions as 20,512, probably garbled; likely a 28,5xx level) and price ran aggressively into it.
- **Timestamps:** [0:00:47](https://youtu.be/KHkUPAsdyfk?t=47) — daily relative equal low, midnight open, unrealized draw; [0:05:43](https://youtu.be/KHkUPAsdyfk?t=343) — minor SSL pool just above the daily draw
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** Related: week-long chain-of-custody videos (L26-047/048/049) using the daily array lows.

### After London relative equal lows and a 7:00 retake: don't expect a return to the midnight open; target the relative equal highs  (aliases ICT uses: short-covering rally, "clean up the relative equal highs")
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:**
  - Price took the daily relative equal low after 7:00; then "massive short covering" (he jokes the whole internet had the same target) took price back above and cleaned up the relative equal highs.
  - The rally came "real short" of the midnight open and fell short.
  - Rule: whenever we go into London with relative equal lows, and right before 7:00 get that low taken once more, do not hold with the expectation of going back all the way to the opening price; it can, but many times it won't. Target the relative equal highs instead and take some profit there.
- **How to identify:** London relative equal lows -> sweep right before/at 7:00 -> reversal.
- **How to use:** after the sweep and reversal, target the relative equal highs (not the midnight open); bank partial profits; consider being done for the day.
- **Example in this video:** NQ 5m/1m Friday: sweep of the daily relative equal lows at the 7:00 acceleration; rally to the relative equal highs; stops just below the midnight open; sell-off again.
- **Timestamps:** [0:04:05](https://youtu.be/KHkUPAsdyfk?t=245) — what I am teaching: don't hold for the open; [0:04:55](https://youtu.be/KHkUPAsdyfk?t=295) — take profit at the relative equal highs
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** A refinement of using the midnight open as a retracement target: it is a possibility, not a requirement.

### Order block validation: no close below required  (aliases ICT uses: order block, change in state of delivery)
- **Kind:** refinement
- **What ICT says:** A fair value gap formed on the move up; price waded up, failed to get bodies to its CE, sold off. For the order block (on the financial chart) the change in the state of delivery occurs once price passes through that candle (the OB's open); it need not close below. He says other teachers claim "it's got to get a close. No, it does not." "Your narrative has to be correct" for it to matter.
- **How to use:** treat trading through the OB's open as the CISD; sell the retrace into the gap/OB; price rallies one more time then gives up.
- **Example in this video:** NQ 1m Friday morning: FVG forms on the retrace up; bodies fail to reach its CE; sell-off through the OB candle; one more rally into it then the final break lower.
- **Timestamps:** [0:04:55](https://youtu.be/KHkUPAsdyfk?t=295) — CISD, no close required, others wrong
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** Same rule as stated in L26-049 (trading down through the OB's open is enough).

## Rules, checklists & numbers stated
- Pre-market session: 7:00-9:00 a.m. ET (informal start 6:30; he reads the market 6:30-7:00).
- London killzone ends at 5:00 a.m. (20 minutes left at ~4:40).
- Bearish normal Power of Three: open at midnight, rally above, then drop lower.
- Don't expect the midnight open to be revisited after London relative equal lows are re-taken right before 7:00; target the relative equal highs.
- OB/CISD: it does not need a close below the open - trading through suffices.
- Daily draw: relative equal lows on NQ daily; minor SSL above it (caption price 20,512, unclear).

## Glossary candidates
`FULCRUM (MIDNIGHT OPEN)` — the midnight ET open price as pivot/bookend of the daily range expansion.
`PRE-MARKET SESSION` — 7:00-9:00 a.m. ET (read from 6:30).
`SHORT-COVERING RALLY` — reversal after the draw is hit, taking the relative equal highs (often not back to the open).

## Summary
Friday May 13, 2022, E-mini Nasdaq (NQ) June 2022. After a week of decline, ICT frames a counter-trend Friday retracement toward relative equal highs at 12,553.25 (draw on liquidity) — stressing that the draw doesn't have to be reached for the setup to pay. The NY open at 9:30 took the overnight London low (power of three), rallied to a 12:10 lunch high, then at the 1:30 PM liquidity search price fell to the day's equilibrium and into a fair value gap around 2:00–2:25 PM, where he bought on the 2‑minute order block + FVG. He shows his executions (including a phone-trading mistake) and his paper account (100k → ~354k since May 6). The second half teaches how to backtest: annotate charts with first-person "I saw this coming" commentary to build pseudo-experience in the subconscious.

## Concepts

### Draw on liquidity doesn't need to be reached
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** If you believe price will draw toward an objective (today or into next week), look for a setup that lets you participate in the expansion toward it; the DOL "does not need to be traded to" to have a profitable framework. Think of it as a magnet.
- **Example in this video:** NQ daily/1h: relative equal highs at 12,547 and 12,553.25 (he uses the higher, 12,553.25, flagged in his community post as "too clean"). Price never reached it on the day, yet the long was profitable.
- **Timestamps:** [0:00:17](https://youtu.be/gLK4Qm6jte8?t=17) — 12,553 too clean; [0:01:54](https://youtu.be/gLK4Qm6jte8?t=114) — DOL needn't be traded to; [0:03:27](https://youtu.be/gLK4Qm6jte8?t=207) — magnet.

### Sell-side taken → buy-side becomes the draw (hourly framework)
- **Kind:** example / rule
- **What ICT says:** On the 1h, a shift in market structure, then relative equal lows were swept (below and back above), one more attempt lower was rejected. With the large sell-side pool taken and price back above the relative equal lows, the clear draw is buy-side above the relative equal highs. Little imbalance remained in between because that area was already rebalanced, so he expected price to "punch through" and take the buy stops.
- **Timestamps:** [0:02:41](https://youtu.be/gLK4Qm6jte8?t=161) — 1h sweep of relative equal lows; [0:03:27](https://youtu.be/gLK4Qm6jte8?t=207) — buy-side is the draw.

### Power of Three at the 9:30 open (London low raid)
- **Kind:** example only
- **What ICT says:** 9:30 manipulation took out the overnight London low (sell-side), created the low of the day, then rallied — accumulate longs, manipulate early longs out by taking overnight stops, then expansion.
- **Example in this video:** NQ 15m/5m, May 13: consolidation, drop at 9:30 through the London low, low of day, rally. (He was unavailable then; no trades.)
- **Timestamps:** [0:04:15](https://youtu.be/gLK4Qm6jte8?t=255); [0:05:01](https://youtu.be/gLK4Qm6jte8?t=301).

### Lunch high and the 1:30 PM afternoon liquidity search
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** The high formed at the 12:10 candle — lunchtime "usually creates a retracement of some sort or consolidation." Around 1:30 PM the algorithm starts seeking liquidity again; if it's going to continue higher, it first seeks sell-side. Relative equal lows existed, but they weren't in a discount yet — the afternoon retracement must reach equilibrium of the day's range (low of day to high of day; purple dashed line = Fib 50%) or below.
- **How to use:** On a bullish day, wait for the afternoon retracement to drop to/below the day's equilibrium into an FVG, then buy for continuation.
- **Example in this video:** NQ 5m: price dropped through equilibrium into an FVG at ~2:00 PM (2:05–2:25 candles), then displaced higher — an MSS within a likely bullish day.
- **Timestamps:** [0:05:49](https://youtu.be/gLK4Qm6jte8?t=349) — buy discount FVG at/below equilibrium; 12:10 lunch high; [0:06:37](https://youtu.be/gLK4Qm6jte8?t=397) — 1:30 liquidity search, equilibrium; [0:07:24](https://youtu.be/gLK4Qm6jte8?t=444) — drop into FVG at 2 PM, displacement.

### Counter-trend (HTF) retracement logic — Friday position squaring
- **Kind:** rule / context
- **What ICT says:** This was counter-trend to the higher timeframe; the logic is that after being down a long time, a retracement is likely, and on Friday most participants want to get out before the weekend, so the algorithm squeezes them to square positions before the Friday close. You *can* frame his FVG model inside a counter-trend idea (if you're a contrarian by nature), but for the highest probability, trade in sync with the early-session move using the 8:30–9:30 window principles.
- **Timestamps:** [0:07:24](https://youtu.be/gLK4Qm6jte8?t=444) — counter-trend to HTF; [0:08:10](https://youtu.be/gLK4Qm6jte8?t=490) — Friday squeeze; [0:13:43](https://youtu.be/gLK4Qm6jte8?t=823) — counter-trend vs 8:30–9:30 highest probability.

### Timeframe step-down to the entry (4m → 3m → 2m) and OB + FVG confluence
- **Kind:** entry model application
- **What ICT says:** 4m: only a small FVG, not used. 3m: a gap visible — "fine if that's what you were looking for." 2m: down-close candles (order block) plus an FVG; price traded down into it — that's where he bought.
- **How to use:** Scale in at the beginning (top) of the FVG and around its middle; he tried for the lower portion but price didn't get there.
- **Example in this video:** 36 contracts bought (paper; assumes a low-margin discount broker such as AMP Futures). Took 10 off on the rally; intended to close 20 with a limit above 12,430 but it closed at market (phone/TradingView error); closed more, final contract late in day. None of the exits were above 12,430.
- **Timestamps:** [0:08:10](https://youtu.be/gLK4Qm6jte8?t=490) — 4m/3m; [0:08:58](https://youtu.be/gLK4Qm6jte8?t=538) — 2m OB + FVG, 36 contracts; [0:10:33](https://youtu.be/gLK4Qm6jte8?t=633) — buying beginning and middle of FVG; [0:11:19](https://youtu.be/gLK4Qm6jte8?t=679) — exits and error.

### Backtesting with first-person annotations (pseudo-experience)
- **Kind:** rule (study method)
- **What ICT says:** Backtesting = going back and marking up old moves (bar replay or hindsight). Fill empty chart space with commentary written as if you saw it coming ("I saw this setting up… I wanted to see this displacement… I expected it to move aggressively toward the short-term highs and took partials above halfway high to low"). Screenshot/print into your journal and reread weekly. This "tricks" the subconscious into forming pseudo-memories and activates the reticular activating system (like noticing your new car everywhere), so live setups feel familiar. Time to full understanding varies — weeks to a year+.
- **Timestamps:** [0:14:30](https://youtu.be/gLK4Qm6jte8?t=870) — what backtesting is; [0:15:16](https://youtu.be/gLK4Qm6jte8?t=916) — annotate with self-talk; [0:17:39](https://youtu.be/gLK4Qm6jte8?t=1059) — reticular activating system; [0:18:26](https://youtu.be/gLK4Qm6jte8?t=1106) — pseudo-memories.
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** He borrowed this from coping techniques he learned for anxiety; frames it as positive self-talk.

### Profit scaling at premium of the swing
- **Kind:** rule (mentioned in the annotation example)
- **What ICT says:** Take something off once price is above halfway between the swing high and low — "premium profit scaling."
- **Timestamps:** [0:17:39](https://youtu.be/gLK4Qm6jte8?t=1059).

## Rules, checklists & numbers stated
- DOL: NQ relative equal highs 12,553.25 (12,547 lower one). Need not be reached.
- 9:30 ET manipulation took the overnight London low.
- Lunch (~12:00–12:10) → retracement/consolidation; 1:30 PM → algorithm seeks liquidity again.
- Afternoon long only at/below equilibrium of the day's range (discount), into an FVG — here ~2:00–2:25 PM.
- Entry TF found on 2m (OB + FVG); buy top and middle of FVG.
- Highest probability = trade with the early session move around 8:30–9:30; counter-trend is an option for contrarians.

## Glossary candidates
`Too clean` — relative equal highs/lows so obvious they are a liquidity magnet.
`Pseudo-experience / pseudo-memories` — familiarity built by annotating backtests as if you anticipated them.
`Premium profit scaling` — taking partials once price is above 50% of the range in a long.

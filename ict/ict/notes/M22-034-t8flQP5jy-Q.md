## Concepts

### Sunday opening gap as a weekly dynamic level (precursor to NWOG)
- **Kind:** new framing / rule
- **What ICT says:** Plot the Sunday opening gap (the gap between Friday close and the Sunday evening reopen, ~6 PM ET) and extend it across the entire week. Many times there's "a lot of valuation around that gap" — it acts as a dynamic price level: if price is above it, look for it to act as support; if below, resistance. Some weeks run away from it and never return, but in consolidation weeks (e.g., an FOMC week) the choppy range-bound action keeps revisiting it. If price is going to trade down into the Sunday gap, look for it to first run above nearby swing highs / relative equal highs (buy-side).
- **How to identify:** Mark Friday's close and Sunday's open; shade the gap; extend right.
- **How to use:** Expect reactions at the gap's edges (he watched its lower end); combine with FVG/OB entries; in consolidation weeks use it as the range's pivot.
- **Example in this video:** ES: price traded below sell-side and into/through the Sunday gap down to an FVG, rallied to the opening range; at lunch retraced back up into the Sunday gap which acted as resistance; dropped to an order block (big down candle), accumulated, rallied back above the gap; retraced back into it as support (discount low); then FVG → 15m OB → run to 3952.75/3954.50.
- **Timestamps:** [0:04:48](https://youtu.be/t8flQP5jy-Q?t=288) — lower end of Sunday gap to watch, resistance from below; [0:05:35](https://youtu.be/t8flQP5jy-Q?t=335) — lunch retracement into gap as resistance; [0:08:48](https://youtu.be/t8flQP5jy-Q?t=528) — "dynamic support/resistance" (and "blasphemy" joke); [0:09:36](https://youtu.be/t8flQP5jy-Q?t=576) — rule above = support, below = resistance; run highs before trading down into it; [0:10:25](https://youtu.be/t8flQP5jy-Q?t=625) — FOMC-week consolidation.

### Fibonacci on the impulse swing in the trade direction (premium/discount correction)
- **Kind:** rule (correction of a common error)
- **What ICT says:** Many new students measure a range in the wrong direction (e.g., a high-to-low leg) and use its 50% as a discount for a long. Wrong. For a long, draw the Fib from the low up to the high of the *impulsive (expansion) swing in the direction you want to trade*; the anticipated retracement to and below 50% is the discount — hunt longs at that level or lower, ideally in an FVG. For a short, measure the down leg (high→low) and sell at/above 50%. "You don't want to use a price leg from high to low measuring something you're trying to go long on."
- **Example in this video:** ES 5m: low to high of the rally toward the relative equal highs/3953 target; retrace below 50% into an FVG = long. Same on a second dealing range (anchor high, run to low that hadn't been taken).
- **Timestamps:** [0:15:18](https://youtu.be/t8flQP5jy-Q?t=918) — the error (from a student's forex video); [0:16:04](https://youtu.be/t8flQP5jy-Q?t=964) — low→high for longs; [0:16:52](https://youtu.be/t8flQP5jy-Q?t=1012) — use impulsive swings in trade direction.

## Rules, checklists & numbers stated
- Sunday opening gap (≈6 PM ET reopen): extend all week; above = support, below = resistance; most useful in consolidation/FOMC weeks.
- Before trading down into the Sunday gap, expect a run above nearby swing/relative equal highs.
- Fib: anchor on the impulse swing in the trade direction; long at/below 50% (discount), short at/above 50% (premium), preferably in an FVG.
- Levels: 5m FVG 3926.75 (perfect delivery); old high 3952.75 / target 3953; high 3954.50; next: 15m gap above, daily OB low 4000.

## Glossary candidates
`Sunday opening gap / gap opening` — the weekly reopen gap on Sunday evening; treated as a dynamic weekly level (later formalized as NWOG).
`Sloppy` — price action offering few clean entry mechanics.
`Missing data` — an actual price gap (not just an FVG) that the market tends to revisit.
`Impulse / expansion swing` — the leg in the trade direction used to measure premium/discount.

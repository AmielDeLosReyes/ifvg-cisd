## Summary
ICT follows up Episode 19, where he said that if he were making the market he would push DXY below 100 to about 99.92–99.95 to set up a dollar buy. In the next London session DXY dropped close to that level, then rallied through relative equal highs, while EURUSD made its high of the day in the London killzone by running the previous week's high: a false breakout. He uses this to answer viewers asking whether the model works in London for forex: intermarket inverse correlation (DXY vs EURUSD) acts as the "barometer," and a 2-minute FVG after displacement through a short-term low gives a limit-order short with ~5 pips of heat. He stresses that this is not an addition to the YouTube model, just an answer to questions, and repeats fixed-percentage risk (≤1%, ideally 0.25–0.5%).

## Concepts

### Market-maker level projection ("if I were making the market")  (aliases: draw it out in time, eyeballed extension)
- **Kind:** example / refinement
- **What ICT says:** His 99.95–99.92 DXY level (a ~3-pip zone) was just eyeballed: he took the run-up at the beginning of a specific prior candle and drew that level out in time, ending the extension on that candle's high. He has other tools ("in my repertoire," taught privately, not here) that fine-tune the low of the day to within 2–3 pips at midnight, but says they're not needed.
- **How to use:** Anticipate where the market maker would take price to create the Judas move/low before the real move (here: below the round 100 level to convince everyone the dollar topped).
- **Example in this video:** DXY: high of the day set around the midnight time frame, then dropped through 100, creating the London low "real close" to 99.92 (not exact, "good enough for government work"), then rallied, took out the initial high of the day and ran through relative equal highs.
- **Timestamps:** [0:01:37](https://youtu.be/Q6GFu8-Z4rY?t=97) — the call from Episode 19 (at ~20:10 of that video); [0:03:58](https://youtu.be/Q6GFu8-Z4rY?t=238) — why 99.92: candle run-up drawn out in time, ~3-pip range; [0:04:46](https://youtu.be/Q6GFu8-Z4rY?t=286) — private tools fine-tune low to 2–3 pips (not taught); [0:05:34](https://youtu.be/Q6GFu8-Z4rY?t=334) — London low formed near the level.
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** He explicitly says this isn't part of the YouTube model and he won't do London live sessions.

### Relative equal highs as intraday target (inefficient "clean" levels)  (aliases: REH, clean highs, retail resistance)
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** Very clean, level highs (three touches here) are "not efficient" from the algorithm's view; retail sees them as perfect resistance because books teach that, so the market runs them. DXY targeted these as the intraday/daily objective after the London low.
- **Timestamps:** [0:05:34](https://youtu.be/Q6GFu8-Z4rY?t=334) — ran above REH as intraday target; [0:06:22](https://youtu.be/Q6GFu8-Z4rY?t=382) — "the market does not like to leave that"; [0:07:57](https://youtu.be/Q6GFu8-Z4rY?t=477) — REH swept as daily objective.

### London Open Killzone  (aliases: London open, London killzone)
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** London open killzone = 2:00 to 5:00 a.m. New York local time; set TradingView's session/time tool to New York. In this example DXY made its low of the day and EURUSD its high of the day inside this window. If price hasn't retraced to your entry yet, you can wait as long as the killzone is still open.
- **Example in this video:** DXY 15m: low of day in London open → runs up into an order block + OTE (he says check lower timeframes to see it's an order block) → runs the REH. EURUSD 15m: high of day inside the killzone.
- **Timestamps:** [0:07:57](https://youtu.be/Q6GFu8-Z4rY?t=477) — killzone 2–5 a.m. NY, DXY low of day, OB + OTE; [0:11:52](https://youtu.be/Q6GFu8-Z4rY?t=712) — EURUSD high of day in killzone; [0:14:15](https://youtu.be/Q6GFu8-Z4rY?t=855) — "the kill zone for London open is still open," so wait for retracement.

### Intermarket analysis: DXY as barometer / false breakout  (aliases: inverse relationship, teeter-totter effect, trading market vs barometer)
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** In forex your trading market (e.g., EURUSD) needs a barometer to measure its movement: the dollar index. Their relationship is inverted. If DXY is going down to a level you've identified as a bullish/buying point, then EURUSD rallying at that moment is likely a *false breakout* and becomes a shorting opportunity at the exact moment DXY hits its level.
- **How to use:** Mark the DXY level where you expect a dollar low; watch the EURUSD high at the same moment (here, taking out the previous week's high = buy stops). Once DXY reaches its level, look for a short in EURUSD.
- **Example in this video:** As DXY hit the ~99.92 area, EURUSD ran above the previous week's high (cleared buy stops) — same instant — then reversed lower.
- **Timestamps:** [0:08:44](https://youtu.be/Q6GFu8-Z4rY?t=524) — EURUSD runs previous week's high, why it's a false breakout; [0:09:30](https://youtu.be/Q6GFu8-Z4rY?t=570) — barometer and inverted relationship; [0:10:17](https://youtu.be/Q6GFu8-Z4rY?t=617) — same moment DXY hit the buy level; [0:15:50](https://youtu.be/Q6GFu8-Z4rY?t=950) — teeter-totter: dollar higher puts weight on euro.
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** He didn't take either trade (asleep, and not trading forex now) — he doesn't need every move.

### Displacement requirement for the FVG entry (which low to use)  (aliases: energetic move away, displacement, parabolic gap)
- **Kind:** rule / refinement
- **What ICT says:** He uses the short-term low that the *displacement* breaks, not a nearer low, because the FVG needs price to move energetically *away* from it. If after the FVG candle closes the next candle opens right at the low and hasn't moved away, there's no displacement yet. A limit order is not placed while price is still inside/near the gap area; it's placed only after price has displaced away from the FVG.
- **How to identify:** Run on buy stops (here, above old weekly high) → price drops through a short-term low (market structure shift) with an energetic candle leaving a FVG → price moves away from the gap.
- **How to use:** Then put a limit order at the FVG (for a short, at the top edge / the high of the candle that defines it). Don't chase if it goes deeper first; wait for the retracement while the killzone is open.
- **Example in this video:** EURUSD 2m, London session (day after Ep. 19, ~April 21, 2022): after running above the old weekly high, price trades lower and takes the short-term low with a FVG; MSS; price goes a bit lower without retracing, then rallies back into the FVG = entry.
- **Timestamps:** [0:12:39](https://youtu.be/Q6GFu8-Z4rY?t=759) — why that low: next candle opened right at it, no displacement yet; [0:13:27](https://youtu.be/Q6GFu8-Z4rY?t=807) — limit order only after price moves away from the FVG.

### London short entry with exact numbers (EURUSD 2m)  (aliases: limit order at FVG, stop above high)
- **Kind:** example only / entry model
- **What ICT says:** Fill could be one pipette, half a pip, or a full pip above the FVG candle's high; he just uses the candle high. Hypothetical limit sell entry 1.09244; stop above the high at 1.09365; the maximum heat after entry was up to 1.09301 (≈5 pips of drawdown) and it never came near the stop.
- **How to use:** Stop above the swing high that ran the buy stops. First target: 50% of the parent price leg (see below); if holding for the day, the lower imbalance.
- **Timestamps:** [0:14:15](https://youtu.be/Q6GFu8-Z4rY?t=855) — entry 1.09244, stop 1.09365; [0:15:02](https://youtu.be/Q6GFu8-Z4rY?t=902) — drawdown to 1.09301 ≈ 5 pips.

### Targets via the parent price leg (Fib 50%) and imbalance  (aliases: parent price leg, first target, hold for full day)
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** On the 5m (only used to see the whole fractal on one chart), draw a Fibonacci on the parent price leg starting from the *most energetic* low that began the run up; the 50% level is a good first target. If holding for the full day, the next objective was a lower imbalance, which price traded into "rather handsomely."
- **Timestamps:** [0:16:37](https://youtu.be/Q6GFu8-Z4rY?t=997) — 5m overview, Fib from most energetic low, 50% = first target; [0:17:24](https://youtu.be/Q6GFu8-Z4rY?t=1044) — full-day hold into the imbalance.

## Rules, checklists & numbers stated
- London open killzone: 2:00–5:00 a.m. New York local time.
- DXY "market maker" low projection: 99.95–99.92 (~3 pips), drawn from a prior candle's run-up extended in time.
- EURUSD entry 1.09244 (limit, at the FVG candle high), stop 1.09365, max heat to 1.09301 (~5 pips).
- Allow fill a pipette / half pip / one pip beyond the FVG edge.
- Don't place the limit order until price has displaced away from the FVG.
- Don't chase if price goes deeper before retracing; wait while the killzone is still open.
- First target: 50% of the parent price leg (from the most energetic origin); full-day target: next imbalance.
- EURUSD moves opposite DXY; a EURUSD high at the moment DXY hits its buy level = likely false breakout.
- Risk ≤1% of equity per trade; preferably 0.5% or 0.25% while learning.

## Glossary candidates
`Barometer (market)` — correlated reference market (DXY) used to judge the trading market (EURUSD).
`Trading market` — the instrument you actually trade, measured against the barometer.
`Teeter-totter effect` — ICT's name for DXY vs foreign-currency inverse correlation.
`Parent price leg` — the whole swing from the most energetic origin, used for the 50% first target.
`Good enough for government work` — ICT phrase: close-enough precision is acceptable.

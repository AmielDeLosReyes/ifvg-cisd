## Summary
A short real-time demo trade in which ICT reads institutional order flow intraday, deliberately *against* the daily (bearish) bias. Sell-side liquidity below relative equal lows (and yesterday's low) has been taken and price has traded into a fair value gap near the 4000 level, so he buys, targeting the buy-side liquidity above a short-term high, with 4051.25 (high of day) as a best-case stretch target. He pyramids by adding on a freshly drawn bullish order block (the higher of two down-close candles), manages the stop under the FVG/old short-term high, scales out four contracts at market above the buy-side, and gets stopped on the final two. The lesson: tape reading means calling and annotating levels *before* price reaches them; the order flow and liquidity pools are what you trade, not the P&L.

## Concepts

### Intraday order flow against the daily bias (after sell-side is taken)
- **Kind:** rule / example
- **What ICT says:** He is trading against the daily bias on the basis that sell-side liquidity has already been taken and price has traded into a fair value gap (~4000). Price traded down three times intraday and took out yesterday's low; the lack of follow-through bolsters a counter-trend long. Retail sees the consolidation as a bear flag and expects lower prices.
- **How to identify:** Relative equal lows / previous day low swept; price reached into a discount FVG; repeated failure to continue lower.
- **How to use:** Target the nearest buy-side liquidity (short-term high), not a full reversal. The best case (high of day) is optional — "it doesn't need to do that to be profitable."
- **Example in this video:** Likely ES (levels ~4000; instrument not named in captions), date not stated. Sell-side below relative equal lows run; entry long around the FVG near 4000 (fill read as ~4006.25/4006.50, caption unclear); draw = buy-side above a short-term high (≈4036 area); stretch = 4051.25 (high of day, buy stops).
- **Timestamps:** [0:00:05](https://youtu.be/k26euOG6zAk?t=5) — against daily bias, sell stops taken, FVG near 4000; [0:01:44](https://youtu.be/k26euOG6zAk?t=104) — SSL taken, 4051 stretch; [0:04:06](https://youtu.be/k26euOG6zAk?t=246) — three drives down, yesterday's low taken, lack of follow-through.

### Bullish order block (drawn in real time) — higher of two down-close candles
- **Kind:** definition / entry refinement
- **What ICT says:** With two consecutive down-close candles, draw the order block on the *higher* of the two; use its uppermost opening price as the level. Price may drop into it, pick up momentum, and the algorithm reprices higher to buy-side liquidity (≈4036). It might only touch the small wick high of that candle, or not touch it at all. Once working, "the down-close candles are supporting price."
- **How to use:** Add to the position (pyramid) when price trades back to the OB. He added two contracts on the first OB (to 6 contracts), then saw price touch the second OB's wick.
- **Timestamps:** [0:04:06](https://youtu.be/k26euOG6zAk?t=246) — adds two on order block, now six long; [0:08:45](https://youtu.be/k26euOG6zAk?t=525) — higher of two down-close candles, uppermost open; [0:10:20](https://youtu.be/k26euOG6zAk?t=620) — touches the wick.

### Stop placement inside FVG / below old short-term high
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** Stop moved up just under a low that "should not trade back down into" — it sits inside the lower end of the fair value gap and below the old short-term high around 9:30. Market structure supports the idea, sell-side is already taken, and price is back inside the range between the stop and the buy-side target. "Notice I'm not in a hurry to rush my stop-loss up." After expansion, stop to breakeven, then a bit higher.
- **Timestamps:** [0:04:52](https://youtu.be/k26euOG6zAk?t=292) — why the stop placement is confident; [0:11:07](https://youtu.be/k26euOG6zAk?t=667) — stop to breakeven and higher.

### Fading the "imbalance" crowd after the 8:30 drop is rebalanced
- **Kind:** example only
- **What ICT says:** Traders who follow imbalances (popular lately) saw the quick sudden drop at 8:30; the market came back and rebalanced it and is now drifting lower, so they try to sell short — he goes against that and targets their stops (buy-side).
- **Timestamps:** [0:09:32](https://youtu.be/k26euOG6zAk?t=572).

### Scaling out & pyramiding (trade management)
- **Kind:** rule / example
- **What ICT says:** Bulk of the position (4 of 6 contracts) comes off at market once price runs above the buy-side liquidity; then consider moving the stop up on the remainder and "see if I can get lucky." He wants delivery "in big candles, speed delivery" toward the target.
- **Example in this video:** Executions: bought the FVG after sell stops taken, added at the order block, sold 4 above the buy-side high, moved stop up and the final 2 were stopped out. ~$5,000+ hypothetical profit.
- **Timestamps:** [0:06:24](https://youtu.be/k26euOG6zAk?t=384) — take four off above BSL at market; [0:12:48](https://youtu.be/k26euOG6zAk?t=768) — executions recap; [0:13:41](https://youtu.be/k26euOG6zAk?t=821) — final two stopped.

### Tape reading practice (no position required)
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** To practice reading order flow you don't need a demo position; mark the liquidity pools and PD arrays before price gets there and state exactly what you expect — "nothing ambiguous." Dim levels already used (e.g., sell-side once taken) so focus stays on active levels. Ignore the P&L — "that's the distraction, pay attention to the price."
- **Timestamps:** [0:03:19](https://youtu.be/k26euOG6zAk?t=199) — P&L is a distraction; [0:07:58](https://youtu.be/k26euOG6zAk?t=478) — no demo position needed, be specific; dim used levels.

## Rules, checklists & numbers stated
- Draw on liquidity = buy-side above short-term high; 4051.25 high-of-day = best case only.
- Bullish OB: the higher of two down-close candles; key level = its opening price; price may only wick into it.
- Stop: inside lower end of the FVG / below old short-term high near 9:30; don't rush it up.
- Scale: 4 contracts off at market above buy-side; trail remainder.
- Position built from ~4 to 6 contracts (pyramiding).
- TradingView tip: hold Shift while dragging a trend line to keep it horizontal.

## Process, risk & psychology
- Don't watch P&L; watch price. [0:03:19](https://youtu.be/k26euOG6zAk?t=199)
- Accept being wrong: if price returns below the stop level "I'm wrong and that's fine." [0:04:52](https://youtu.be/k26euOG6zAk?t=292)
- Call it before it happens, precisely, to prove understanding. [0:07:58](https://youtu.be/k26euOG6zAk?t=478)

## Glossary candidates
`Caffeine bar` — ICT's name for big green (bullish) candles showing speed/expansion when long.
`Tape reading / reading order flow` — real-time anticipation of price to marked liquidity and PD arrays, with levels drawn before price arrives.
`Bull's-eye` — the target level (here the buy-side liquidity high).

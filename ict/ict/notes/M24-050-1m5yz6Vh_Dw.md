## Summary
Recorded Sunday Dec 1 2024 at about 4:10 PM ET, ahead of the 6 PM Sunday open, this is ICT's weekly game plan for NQ. His higher-timeframe narrative is bullish (stock indices); he is "trying to avoid picking a top" and favors a run above November's high. The upside objective is 21580.25, taken from the range's retracement leg with an extension of about a quarter of the range above the broken high. A daily-chart gap, which would become an inversion FVG if price breaks higher and returns, is the potential catalyst. He reviews last week's NWOG reactions at the upper quadrant, CE and lower quadrant to show how precisely price respects them, then states which Sunday opens he would like (a softer open that trades down into the NWOG and back above it) and which he would not. He ends with the NFP-week rule for newer traders: do your work Monday and Tuesday and be finished by the New York open on Wednesday.

## Concepts

- **Kind:** rule / example
- **What ICT says:**

  - He favors a run higher and is "trying to avoid picking a top". If price keeps moving higher he expects a break of November's high, and one level to look for is 21580.25 from the retracement-leg-to-high extension (a quarter of the range above the high, which video 51 states explicitly as his rule of thumb).

- **How to use:** if November's high is taken, target the extension level; ignore the minor wick/high on the way ("I'd like to see them completely ignore this wick").
- **Example in this video:** NQ December 2024 contract; objective 21580.25 above November's high.

### Inversion FVG as a catalyst above the range high

### Relative equal highs and a stop run (hourly / 15m)
- **Kind:** example only

A stop run on the 15-minute chart took out a low (with a volume imbalance there), and on the 15m a Wednesday volume imbalance had sell side below it that was hit "beautifully"; relative equal highs were then taken and the next highs are the new targets.

### New Week Opening Gap quadrants (upper quadrant, CE, lower quadrant, low, high) as reaction levels
- **Kind:** example only (refinement of use)
- **What ICT says:** he walks last week's NQ action against the NWOG levels to show the "precision": Monday broke lower and hit sell side; Wednesday price traded back up into the NWOG CE, swept below the upper quadrant (a false break), then broke higher; it respected the CE (twice), the upper quadrant and the lower quadrant, and candle bodies respected the CE; price also traded down into the NWOG high. The inversion FVG (orange area) and a volume imbalance also interacted with these levels. Some phrases in the captions here are garbled (e.g. an OTE reference, caption unclear).
- **How to identify:** divide the NWOG into upper quadrant, CE (midpoint), lower quadrant, plus high and low.
- **How to use:** watch these as support/resistance and reaction levels within the week.

## Glossary candidates

## Summary
First trading day after Memorial Day (likely Tue May 31, 2022 — NFP week, month transition), E-mini S&P (ES) June 2022. On the 1h, price sat in a discount FVG below equilibrium — "oversold" without any indicator. On the 5m/1m he shows how the morning ran to a premium, then aggressively slid through relative equal lows around the 9:30 open with *no model entry* (a sign it was a rush to clear sell-side, not a real short), then shifted structure and delivered up into a deep-premium FVG. He bought a discount FVG after the sell stops were cleared: 5 contracts at 4120, stop below the swing low, partials at 4139.25 and 4143.75 (tweet target 4143.50). Key ideas: which market structure shift counts depends on the range you're working; a failure to take lows at 8:30 news hints at a deep retracement instead; and his weekly discipline — be done by Wednesday in NFP weeks when there's no clear objective.

## Concepts

### Premium/discount on the hourly as "overbought/oversold" (no indicator needed)
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** Take the hourly swing low to swing high; equilibrium is the 50% level; anything above is premium, below is discount. Price dropping into a fair value gap below equilibrium = an "oversold condition" — "do we need an indicator to plot that? Absolutely not."
- **Timestamps:** [0:00:59](https://youtu.be/pWnMpdN_g98?t=59).

### Sell-side clearing with no model entry = "tipping its hand"
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** Before 9:30 price ran up into a premium; then it sank rapidly around the 9:30 opening, taking relative equal lows (sell-side), bounced briefly, then took another low — knocking out longs who tried to pick the bottom. There was no model short entry on that drop: "that's telling, it's tipping its hand" — it was a rush to clear sell-side liquidity, not a genuine bearish move. Retail rarely re-enters after being stopped like that.
- **How to identify:** Fast drop through obvious lows without a displacement/FVG setup that your model would short.
- **How to use:** Treat it as a liquidity sweep in a bullish context; look for the subsequent MSS + FVG long.
- **Timestamps:** [0:01:47](https://youtu.be/pWnMpdN_g98?t=107) — run to premium then slide through relative equal lows; [0:05:42](https://youtu.be/pWnMpdN_g98?t=342) — no model entry, tipping its hand.

### 8:30 failure to take the lows → deep retracement, not reversal
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** He didn't believe certain lows would be taken because, if they were going to be, it should have happened at the 8:30 news — it didn't, so the pullback was just a deep retracement before price was sent higher into a premium before 9:30.
- **Timestamps:** [0:05:42](https://youtu.be/pWnMpdN_g98?t=342).

### Which market structure shift counts? (depends on your range)
- **Kind:** refinement
- **What ICT says:** Students see multiple short-term structure breaks and ask which is "the" MSS. Answer: it depends on the range you're working within. He was playing the run up to a premium (as tweeted), so in that range the MSS occurs when price takes out *that* range's relevant high, then drops into the FVG — which is what he bought.
- **Timestamps:** [0:04:55](https://youtu.be/pWnMpdN_g98?t=295); [0:05:42](https://youtu.be/pWnMpdN_g98?t=342).

### Long after sell stops are cleared (5m/1m execution)
- **Kind:** entry model application
- **What ICT says:** Sequence: MSS → rally (rebalancing the drop) → 5m FVG → draw on the morning high (short-term highs) → overshoot into a deep-premium FVG → pullback inside the range → FVG, MSS, relative equal highs → drop into a very good order block → rally into imbalance → consolidation, bump above short-term high → final drop clearing sell-side → his long. "I'm buying those sell stops."
- **Example in this video:** ES 1m: long 5 contracts @ 4120 at a discount; ~5 handles of heat; stop below the swing low (not hit). Tweet: "sell-side liquidity taken, free to run to 4143.50." 3 contracts sold at 4139.25 (first partial, when tweeted); final 2 filled by limit at 4143.75; price later went a bit higher. Earlier small morning trade with partials and a limit fill also shown.
- **Timestamps:** [0:02:34](https://youtu.be/pWnMpdN_g98?t=154) — MSS, 5m FVG, morning high as DOL; [0:04:07](https://youtu.be/pWnMpdN_g98?t=247) — long at discount, 5 handles heat, stop below swing low; [0:06:30](https://youtu.be/pWnMpdN_g98?t=390) — entry 4120, tweet; [0:07:17](https://youtu.be/pWnMpdN_g98?t=437) — partials 4139.25 / 4143.75.

### Weekly trading discipline (NFP week / month transition)
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** In the first week of the month (non-farm payroll week, transition between months) he typically likes to have all trading done by Wednesday. Having made his trade, he's satisfied for the week. Without a clear definitive objective on the daily (could go either way), he sits on his hands. Consistency comes from rules of engagement.
- **Timestamps:** [0:07:17](https://youtu.be/pWnMpdN_g98?t=437); [0:08:04](https://youtu.be/pWnMpdN_g98?t=484).

### Futures contract months and rollover
- **Kind:** definition
- **What ICT says:** Index futures are delivered by contract month — trading the June 2022 contract in May is normal; in a couple of weeks June trading ends and rolls into the September 2022 contract.
- **Timestamps:** [0:00:08](https://youtu.be/pWnMpdN_g98?t=8).

## Rules, checklists & numbers stated
- Hourly discount (below 50% of swing) into FVG = oversold; no indicator needed.
- A sell-off with no model entry = likely a liquidity clearing move, not a trade.
- If lows weren't taken at 8:30 news, expect a retracement rather than a breakdown.
- MSS is defined relative to the range you're trading.
- Trade: ES long 5 @ 4120; stop below swing low; 3 off @ 4139.25; 2 off @ 4143.75 (target 4143.50).
- NFP week: aim to finish trading by Wednesday; no clear objective → no trading.

## Process, risk & psychology
- Heat of ~5 handles was "bearable"; he didn't watch every tick — "I don't care" once the stop is placed logically. [0:04:07](https://youtu.be/pWnMpdN_g98?t=247)
- Twitter isn't a signal service; tweets are time-stamped proof. [0:01:47](https://youtu.be/pWnMpdN_g98?t=107)
- Discipline: be content after a good trade; sit on hands without a clear objective. [0:08:04](https://youtu.be/pWnMpdN_g98?t=484)

## Glossary candidates
`Tipping its hand` — price revealing its true intent, e.g., a drop without a model entry signals a liquidity run rather than a real reversal.
`Rules of engagement` — personal rules for when to trade (e.g., done by Wednesday in NFP week).
`Contract rollover` — moving from the expiring futures month (June) to the next (September).

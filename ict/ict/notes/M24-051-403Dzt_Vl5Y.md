## Concepts

### One-quarter-of-the-broken-range extension as an upside objective  (aliases: rule of thumb, "easiest draw on liquidity", frame of reference for turtle soup)
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:**
  - Once a range's high is taken out, look for an upside objective: simple, cut-and-dry draw on liquidity, or a frame of reference for a potential turtle soup.
  - His rule of thumb: one quarter of the previous range, projected beyond the broken high, is where he thinks markets can trade to. He says it is not based on anything in technical books, just something he has used over the years.
  - When the market is at all-time highs, you stay with going long until it proves otherwise.
  - The extension target does not need to be hit. Here price made a higher high but failed to reach it (the hourly high stopped just below 21580, at what the captions give as about 21567.25, caption unclear). He says it does not matter, and that it could rip through it later.
  - He told Sunday viewers "this is about as far as I would like to see it trade to" (21580.25).
- **How to identify:** a daily retracement leg (the high down to the retracement low) is the range; draw the extension of one quarter of that range above the old high with the Fib tool.
- **How to use:** draw/frame for targets or turtle-soup reversals at that level.
- **Example in this video:** NQ Dec 2024: Sunday video stated 21580.25 after the November/prior-week high was taken; Thursday traded above the high but stopped short of it.

### Short entry with layered limit orders and market-structure shift off the 8 AM high
- **Kind:** example only (live trade review)
- **What ICT says / what happened:**
  - Price traded up through the 8 AM high; on the rally the candle bodies broke down, a shift in market structure.
  - As price traded back above the old high, he went short; there was an immediate rebalance where price traded back up into the low, and he added there.
  - Price then attacked the level below; he had several limit orders layered below in case price did not reach 21490. Two limit orders filled; one went right to the target price but the spread prevented him exiting on it, and the remaining four contracts were not filled.
  - The market rallied back and hit his stop at about 21526 (caption reads "526"); he gave guidance on that live on X.
  - Price then went into range-bound consolidation.
- **How to use:** layered limit orders below allow for not reaching the ideal level; accept that partial fills and spread can prevent the last contracts from being filled.

### 5-second chart short off a reclaimed SIBI (premium array), then sell-side run to the limit
- **Kind:** entry model (example)
- **What ICT says / what happened:**
  - Price made high, higher high, higher high, then took out that high. In that small range he looks at the inefficiency and volume imbalance on a 5-second chart; candle bodies respect the SIBI's upper wick/high.
  - He went short aiming for the CE of the inefficiency and the volume imbalance. Entries on a 5-second chart are imperfect and he used market orders (buy/sell buttons) rather than limit orders, partly to show fills are real.
  - Price traded lower then back up into the array. He treats it as a premium array: a "reclaimed" SIBI (used once, then used again). Bodies respected the midpoint (CE) and then the high. He saw a bull-flag look that retail would buy and used the reclaimed SIBI to short again.
  - Price broke down and took out the low aggressively; thin price action on the way (versus back-and-forth chop earlier), leaving a nice new inefficiency; price came back up into it ("two of them; always give room for it to trade from the higher one") and then sold off aggressively into the 21490 level, where his limit order was filled.
- **How to identify:** SIBI in a short-term range at a premium; bodies (not just wicks) respecting its high/CE; retail-attractive bull flag pattern right at it.
- **How to use:** short with target at the CE/volume imbalance and the pre-planned level; reclaimed SIBI is a second chance entry.

## Rules, checklists & numbers stated
- Upside extension: one quarter of the previous range beyond the broken high (here 21580.25).
- Called levels: take the 8:00 AM high, drop to 21490, then roll up toward 21580.
- If price is inside one candle's range through the 9:00 hour, wait for a trade to 10:00 before judging.
- Shorting: bodies (not only wicks) must respect the array; reclaimed SIBI is usable again.
- Layer limit orders below if you doubt price will reach your level; not all will fill.
- At all-time highs, stay long until proven otherwise.

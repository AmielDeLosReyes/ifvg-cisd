## Summary
A 5-minute clip in which ICT answers a viewer who asked how to tell his live executions from TradingView Market Replay. He demonstrates that when Replay is switched on, the buy/sell buttons and the time axis change and the live-feed status button disappears, whereas on a live paper-trading feed the buy/sell buttons sit at the top and the status button shows whether the market is open. He then shows a live entry and a partial take-profit as proof, and notes he still has a remainder running toward a "quadruple top" while he leaves the stop in place. There is no concept teaching; the only transferable points are about showing live execution versus replay and a brief remark on how he is managing the open remainder.

## Concepts

### Live paper-trading execution vs Market Replay (verification of live execution)
- **Kind:** example only (platform demonstration, not a trading concept)
- **What ICT says:**
  - He teaches on a paper-trading account (shown at the bottom of the platform) with live data.
  - With Market Replay switched on, the top-bar buy/sell buttons disappear, the spacing between the time and the time axis changes, and the buy/sell controls move to the lower right of the chart.
  - On live data the buy/sell hot buttons stay at the top and the status button shows whether the market is open (hovering over it gives the live-feed timer). That timer cannot be used under Replay, which is why he highlights it and hovers over it.
  - The live entries and the partial take-profit he shows were therefore not pre-recorded.
- **How to identify:** Check the placement of the buy/sell buttons and the live status/timer button.
- **How to use:** n/a (credibility check, not a trading tool).
- **Example in this video:** Replay toggled on and off on the chart, then a live entry and a partial taken in real time.
- **Timestamps:**
  - [0:01:02](https://youtu.be/hfwUXg2nYhs?t=62) — the difference between Replay and live execution explained.
  - [0:02:15](https://youtu.be/hfwUXg2nYhs?t=135) — a live entry and a partial profit shown.
  - [0:03:12](https://youtu.be/hfwUXg2nYhs?t=192) — why the hot buttons and live status button prove it is not Replay.
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** This explains the same hot-button habit he later describes in M24-038 (market orders used on stream only to prove live data).

### Open remainder toward a quadruple top (trade management note)
- **Kind:** example only
- **What ICT says:** After booking the partial, he leaves the remainder to reach up and sweep a level he calls a quadruple top (the printed level reads "9,694.25" in the captions, which looks mis-captioned and no instrument is named, so treat as caption unclear). He has already booked the partial, and the stop stays where it is below; if it comes back and stops him out during his cookout, so be it.
- **How to identify / use:** Quadruple top = multiple equal highs as buy-side liquidity target (his wording).
- **Example in this video:** Live trade, instrument and price unclear in captions; partial taken, remainder still working to the equal highs, stop left in place.
- **Timestamps:** [0:04:50](https://youtu.be/hfwUXg2nYhs?t=290) — remainder, quadruple-top target, stop left in place.
- **Nuance / caveats / changes:** Illustrates partial then hold the remainder with a fixed stop (compare M24-038 on partials).

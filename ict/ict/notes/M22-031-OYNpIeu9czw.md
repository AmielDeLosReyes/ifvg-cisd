## Summary
A short ES (June 2022) review. That morning ICT pointed followers to the May 16, 2022 low as the draw on liquidity ("I'm the scout, you're the sniper") — pointing at a low means aim for its sell-side, not "it shouldn't come back here." He shows a short that was *not* at the open: a 15m bearish order block (last up-close candle), a 5m FVG that price overshot, then a 3‑candle swing high, entering on the next candle with the stop above the high. He trailed the stop quickly because volatility was extreme, took partials, and rode it through the typical lunch consolidation to ~3910. Lesson: if you miss the opening setup, an FVG later in the move still lets you participate toward the draw. Date isn't stated (the day after Ep 30, likely Wed May 18, 2022).

## Concepts

### Pointing to liquidity (scout / sniper framing)
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** He never calls entries/stops/targets; he points to where price is likely to go. When price is above and he draws attention to an old low (the May 16 low), he means it's the target for sell-side liquidity — "we're aiming for liquidity." He's the scout; students are snipers who must do the scoping (entry, stop, limit orders), which he has already taught.
- **Timestamps:** [0:02:30](https://youtu.be/OYNpIeu9czw?t=150) — doesn't call trades; [0:03:19](https://youtu.be/OYNpIeu9czw?t=199) — pointing at May 16 low = aim for liquidity; scout/sniper.

### Using FVGs to join a move after the open
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** Don't fixate only on the setup at the opening. If you don't get a trade at the open, you can still use a fair value gap to get involved with the move toward the draw.
- **Timestamps:** [0:04:52](https://youtu.be/OYNpIeu9czw?t=292).

### Bearish order block (15m) → FVG (5m) → swing high entry
- **Kind:** entry model application
- **What ICT says:** 15m: the up-close candle right before price went lower is a bearish order block; extend it in time — price came back and hit it. 5m: an FVG that isn't visible until you zoom in; price overshoots it (high volatility). Stop goes above that candle's high. Fine-tuning: after hitting the FVG, a three-candle swing high formed; as soon as that third candle closed, he entered short on the next candle — trusting that the FVG was reached, a swing high formed, and bias is bearish, with the May 16 low as the target.
- **How to identify:** Bearish bias + identified SSL target below; 15m last up-close candle (OB) tapped; 5m FVG traded into; 3‑candle swing high confirms.
- **How to use:** Enter after swing high completes; stop above the high; target old low (SSL); partials along the way.
- **Example in this video:** ES 5m: 4 contracts short at 4007.75, stop above the swing high, target below the May 16 low. First partial on the initial drop, another by limit order; final exit around 3910 near the low of the day.
- **Timestamps:** [0:05:40](https://youtu.be/OYNpIeu9czw?t=340) — 15m bearish OB; [0:06:29](https://youtu.be/OYNpIeu9czw?t=389) — 5m FVG overshot, stop above; swing high; [0:07:16](https://youtu.be/OYNpIeu9czw?t=436) — entry logic.

### Trailing stops in high volatility
- **Kind:** rule
- **What ICT says:** Once price broke lower and moved aggressively in his favor, he moved the stop down — only because volatility was so high that a retracement could knock him out. "That's not fear, that's just sound money management." He also lacked time to babysit the trade.
- **Timestamps:** [0:04:52](https://youtu.be/OYNpIeu9czw?t=292) — couldn't babysit; [0:07:16](https://youtu.be/OYNpIeu9czw?t=436) — moved stop due to volatility.

### Lunch consolidation and stair-step delivery
- **Kind:** example only
- **What ICT says:** Price went sideways into the start of New York lunch (typical lunchtime consolidation), ran stops "ahead of lunch," then broke lower in a sequence: consolidation → lower → consolidation → bump stops → sell-off to sell-side → consolidation → low of day.
- **Timestamps:** [0:08:05](https://youtu.be/OYNpIeu9czw?t=485); [0:08:57](https://youtu.be/OYNpIeu9czw?t=537).

### Forex vs index futures (volatility rotation)
- **Kind:** rule (context)
- **What ICT says:** Indices are where the volatility is now; "price is price" — it works the same in forex. He expects currencies to have "pretty wild movement" within ~12 months, at which point indices will look slow. He was long USD/CAD on the 8:30 impact news that morning; it was a "snooze fest."

## Rules, checklists & numbers stated
- Pointing to an old low = target its sell-side liquidity.
- If no opening setup, use a later FVG to join the move.
- Bearish OB = last up-close candle before the down move; extend in time.
- Entry after a 3-candle swing high forms post-FVG tap; stop above that high.
- Trade: ES short 4 contracts @ 4007.75 → target below May 16, 2022 low; exited near 3910.
- Trail stops faster only in very high volatility.

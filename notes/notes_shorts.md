## Dec 2025 - early Mar 2026 (#0-#77): the "4 confirmations" reels
- The model, repeated in ~30 reels (#2 xliH0W5WV-s, #10, #22 LffPMDFFlNY, #47 HBa6qQUpnq0, #54 ...):
  1. Liquidity sweep: "price must take out a major swing high or swing low" (#22). Liquidity = swing highs/lows where stops sit (#64).
  2. Delivering from a higher-time-frame FVG: "15-minute or 1-hour chart" (#47); "15-minute, 1-hour, or 4-hour" (#5); "15-minute and above" (#59);
     must be RESPECTED: "I wait for price to tap and bounce at least once before I trust it" (#9 JkEOnp-pid0, #65). Chopping through it = not respected (#70).
  3. 1-minute FVG inversion: "price must displace and close through a fair value gap" (#2); "not a wick through it, a full candle close on the other side" (#74).
  4. CISD: "close below the last series of up-close candles leading into a liquidity sweep" (#2); "No CISD, no trade" (#35).
  Entry = CISD close (#0, #14, #26). "Three confirmations isn't four" (#26).
- Grades (#34 -TGAiz3K8qY): A+ = 4 confirmations + SMT divergence + clean structure; A = 4 confirmations + clear opposing liquidity;
  B = three confirmations ("I skip these now"); C = two or fewer (#44). "three to five [trades] per week".
- SMT (#1, #12, #24, #42, #52, #62, #73): NQ sweeps, ES does not = divergence; both sweep = "breakout has more conviction", pass (#42).
  "SMT isn't required for every trade ... upgrades your setup from A to A+" (#24). Stop above the SMT high (#1, #12).
- Internal sweep (#13, #21, #69): after the external sweep price makes a small counter-move then sweeps that; best entry, 1:3-1:4.
- Stop: "above the candles that caused the change in the state" (#2); "above the high that was swept for shorts or below the low that was
  swept for longs. Not one tick above ... above the actual sweep" (#25 agNp1kazdB8); "stop below the recent low" (#22); "just past the level that got swept" (#58).
- Target: opposing liquidity = next swing low/high; "at least a 1:2 ... If the math doesn't work, I skip the trade, even if all four confirmations are there" (#23 502fnjYAQRY); "minimum one to two" (#10, #16).
- Entry timing (#33 vIGzxBVbGGo): aggressive at CISD close for A+ with strong momentum; pullback to CISD / inverted FVG for B+ ("Most of the time, price does retrace to CISD").
- Stacked FVGs: all must be closed through before entry (#31 8riL3wKYAmo).
- OTE (#28 kxXTwSlKIL4, #36, #46): 61.8-79% (also marks 50%); fib low->high for bullish, high->low for bearish; "OTE tells you where, the confirmations tell you when"; "If price never gets there, I don't take the trade" (#36).

  "an extremely aggressive move before the sweep ... 100 points"; no clear opposing liquidity. Friday afternoons never (#51).
